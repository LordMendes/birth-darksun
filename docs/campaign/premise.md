# Campaign premise

This campaign uses **Dark Sun 3.5** (Athas.org DS3 baseline) for races, classes, defiling, templars, and geography. One house-rule layer sits on top: **the gods of the Green Age died**, and their death is why Athas is dying.

## The Shattering

At the end of the Green Age, the Athasian pantheon was gathered at [Godshold](../world/landmarks/godshold.md). Something destroyed them there. The exact cause is **unknown** at the table.

When the last gods died, no power remained to **recycle** life through the world. Divine essence did not vanish; it **leaked** and **fractured**.

## The Hemorrhage

Essence soaked into three places:

1. **Primordial legacies** — scion bloodlines (Sun, Storm, Sea, Stone, Mind, Defiler, Dragon)
2. **Covenant** — loyalty and recognition channeled through legacy cults and factions
3. **Source wounds** — defiling nodes, the Gray, dying oases

Athas loses a little more life every season because nothing divine remains to close the wound.

## What this changes

| Topic | Standard Dark Sun | This table |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gods | Absent or unreachable | **Dead** — cults remember portfolios, not living deities |
| PC supernatural powers | Optional psionics | [Legacy powers](../rules/blood-abilities/README.md) + [psionics](../rules/psionics/README.md) |
| Why the world dies | Rajaat, defiling, SKs | **Above**, plus legacy extraction and defiling |
| Sorcerer-kings | Arcane tyrants | Legendary scions (often Defiler, Dragon, or Sun legacy) |

## What stays the same

- Sorcerer-kings, city-states, merchant houses, Tablelands atlas
- Defiling and preserving arcane magic (DS3)
- FY 10 era; 2e map miles for travel

## Player-facing summary

> The gods died at Godshold. Their power broke into **legacies** — sun, storm, stone, mind, and worse. Sorcerer-kings are the greatest scions. You carry a legacy in your blood, the Way in your mind, or both. Power taken from the world has a cost.
