# Aarakocra

> "You are all slaves. You all suffer from the tyranny of the ground. Only in the company of clouds will you find the true meaning of freedom."
> — Kekko Cloud-Brother, aarakocra cleric

## Overview

Aarakocra are the most commonly encountered bird-people of the Tablelands. Some are from [Winter Nest](../world/settlements/winter-nest.md) in the White Mountains near [Kurn](../world/settlements/kurn.md); others come from smaller tribes scattered in the Ringing Mountains and elsewhere. These freedom-loving creatures rarely leave their mountain homes, but young wanderers and cautious adventurers sometimes venture into the inhabited regions.

## Personality

Bird-people can spend hours riding wind currents in the olive-tinged Athasian sky. While traveling, aarakocra prefer to fly high above to get a good view of their location and detect threats in advance. When they rest, they perch on high peaks or tall buildings. Enclosed spaces threaten them — a racial fear of anywhere they cannot stretch their wings. This claustrophobia affects their behavior. Unless absolutely necessary, no aarakocra enters a cave, enclosed building, or narrow canyon. Even good aarakocra rarely help strangers.

## Physical description

Aarakocra stand 6½ to 8 feet tall with a wingspan of about 20 feet. They have black eyes, gray beaks, and from a distance resemble lanky disheveled vultures. Plumage ranges from silver white to brown, even pale blue. Males weigh around 100 pounds; females average 85 pounds. The beak comprises much of the head and can be used in combat. At the center of their wings, aarakocra have three-fingered hands with an opposable thumb; foot talons are equally dexterous. While flying they use feet as hands; while walking they use wing-hands to carry weapons or equipment. A bony chest plate provides protection from blows, but most bones are hollow and brittle. Armor must be made specifically for aarakocra. They usually live between 30 and 40 years.

## Relations

Aarakocra zealously defend their homeland and distrust strangers on their lands. Many southern tribes exact tolls on caravans, sometimes kidnapping scouts or lone riders until tribute is paid — livestock or shiny objects, which aarakocra covet. Some evil tribes attack caravans without provocation. They have great confidence in their ability to fly but little empathy for land-bound races.

## Alignment

Aarakocra tend toward neutrality with regard to law or chaos. With respect to good and evil, tribal alignment usually follows the leader's — a neutral good leader's tribe will contain lawful good, neutral good, chaotic good, and neutral members, with most being neutral good.

## Lands

Most aarakocra communities are small nomadic tribes. Some prey on caravans; others build isolated aeries in the mountains. The least xenophobic generally come from Winter Nest in the White Mountains, allied with [Kurn](../world/settlements/kurn.md). Only Kurn builds perches for aarakocra to rest and do business. King Daskinor of [Eldaarich](../world/settlements/eldaarich.md) has ordered capture and extermination of all aarakocra. Other human communities tolerate aarakocra characters but do not welcome them. Merchants deal with aarakocra who remain on foot. Land-bound creatures are suspicious of creatures that fly over herds or lands unannounced; templars, even in Kurn, have standing orders to attack creatures that fly over city walls without permission.

## Magic

Most aarakocran tribes shun wizardly magic, but a few evil tribes have defilers. One prominent good-aligned tribe, Winter's Nest, has several preservers.

## Psionics

Aarakocra are as familiar with psionics as other Tablelands races. They particularly excel in psychoportation. In spite of low strength and constitution, they excel as psychic warriors, often using ranged touch powers from above.

## Religion

Aarakocran shamans are usually air clerics, sometimes sun clerics, occasionally druids. Most rituals involve summoning an air elemental (*Hraak'thunn* in Auran; aarakocra call their language Silvaarak, not Auran). Summoned air elementals are used in the Hunt — the coming-of-age ceremony involving hunting great beasts of the Silt Sea.

## Language

Athasian aarakocra speak Auran. They have no written language of their own; sophisticated tribes borrow alphabets from land-bound neighbors. Without lips, aarakocra cannot approximate *m*, *b*, or *p* sounds. They struggle with *f* and *v*, tending to pronounce these as *th*.

- **Male:** Akthag, Awnunaak, Cawthra, Driikaak, Gazziija, Kraah, Krekkekelar, Nakaaka, Thraka
- **Female:** Arraako, Kariko, Kekko, Lisako, Troho
- **Tribal:** Cloud Gliders, Sky Divers, Peak Masters, Far Eyes, Brothers of the Sun

## Adventurers

Adventuring aarakocra are usually young adults with a taste for the unknown — curious, strong-minded individuals who wish to experience land-bound life. Good tribes see these as undisciplined but tolerate the behavior. Evil tribes may view it as treacherous and hunt the rogue member.

## Society

Aarakocra have a tribal society. The civilized tribes of Winter Nest form the largest known community in the Tyr region. Communities are led by a chieftain, but aarakocra love personal freedom — the chieftain makes major decisions only after consulting tribal elders and building consensus, or her decisions may be ignored. Air and sun shamans play an important role. They worship the sun for the thermals it provides and hold daily worship of air spirits. Winter Nest aarakocra revere nature's gifts and punish those who despoil the White Mountains. In primitive societies, females rarely travel far from the nest; in Winter Nest, both sexes participate in all aspects of society, with females more often elected chieftains. Aarakocra believe flight makes them superior to all other races. Though they express sympathy for those unable to fly, it often comes across as condescending. Aarakocra are carnivores but do not eat intelligent prey.

## Roleplaying suggestions

Loneliness does not bother you like it bothers other races. You loathe the heat and stink of cities and long for cold, clean mountain air. The spectacle of so many sentient beings fascinates you, but watching from above satisfies your curiosity. The thought of being caught in a crowd, pinned so tight you cannot move your wings, fills you with terror. You are friendly with other races if they respect your distance and approach you first. You form relationships with individuals but avoid other communities' politics — you prefer to watch from above and keep opinions to yourself unless asked. You prefer entering buildings through windows. You keep scattered hidden nests throughout areas you travel regularly. Rely on dive-bombing tactics and a bird's-eye view of every situation.

## Racial traits

- **−2 Strength, +4 Dexterity, −2 Constitution:** Keen reflexes, but lightweight bones are fragile.
- **Monstrous Humanoid:** Not subject to spells or effects that affect humanoids only (e.g. *charm person*, *dominate person*).
- **Medium:** No special bonuses or penalties due to size.
- **Low-light vision:** See twice as far as a human in moonlight and similar poor illumination, retaining color and detail.
- **Speed:** Base land speed 20 feet; fly 90 feet (average maneuverability).
- **+6 racial bonus on Spot checks** in daylight.
- **Natural armor +1** (bone chest plate).
- **Natural weaponry:** Rake with claws for 1d3 damage each; bite for 1d2 as secondary attack.
- **Claustrophobic:** −2 morale penalty on all rolls when in an enclosed space. Underground or enclosed buildings are extremely distressing.
- **Aerial dive:** Dive attacks work like a charge but require minimum 30 feet of movement. Lance attack deals double damage; optionally, full attack with natural weapons (two claws and one bite) at end of charge for normal damage.
- **Automatic languages:** Auran and Common.
- **Bonus languages:** Elven, Gith, Saurian.
- **Favored class:** Cleric (does not count when determining multiclass XP penalty).
- **Level adjustment +1.**
