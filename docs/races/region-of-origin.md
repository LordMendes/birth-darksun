# Region of origin

Where a character comes from can dictate speech, clothing, worldview, and values. In play, cultural differences show up in class, skill, feat, and prestige-class choices. These are **guidelines**, not restrictions — exceptions always exist.

Classes, skills, feats, and prestige classes marked with \* are from DS3 v8. Superscripts: **APX** = Prestige Class Appendixes; **FFN** = Faces of the Forgotten North; **LSH** = Life-Shaping Handbook; **XPH** = Expanded Psionics Handbook. Others are from the Player's Handbook or Dungeon Master's Guide.

## Balic

City-state at the eastern tip of the Balican Peninsula, ruled by a triumvirate of largest merchant houses.

- **Classes:** Bard, gladiator\*, templar\*
- **Skills:** Perform (any)
- **Feats:** Performance Artist\*
- **Prestige classes:** Dune trader\*, master shipfloater APXII, shadow dancer, shadow templar APXI, shadow wizard APXI

## Barrier Wastes

Desolate Jagged Cliffs region; home to the Bandit States (violent humanoid raiding tribes).

- **Classes:** Barbarian, fighter
- **Skills:** Intimidate, Survival
- **Feats:** Intimidating Presence\*, Wastelander\*
- **Prestige classes:** Master scout\*, kik APXII, savage APXI

## Draj

Warrior city-state on vast mud flat east of [Raam](../world/settlements/raam.md); mostly human, intermingled common races.

- **Classes:** Fighter, gladiator\*, templar\*
- **Skills:** Intimidate, Knowledge (nature)
- **Feats:** Astrologer\*, Mekillothead\*
- **Prestige classes:** Arrow knight APXI, cerulean APXI, dune trader\*, eagle knight APXI, jaguar knight APXI, moon priest APXI

## Eldaarich

Small island in the Sea of Silt; ruled by mad Daskinor and ruthless red guards.

- **Classes:** Gladiator\*, templar\*
- **Skills:** Intimidate, Sense Motive
- **Feats:** Grovel FFN, Paranoid\*, Reign of Terror FFN
- **Prestige classes:** Brown cloak WotDL, executioner FFN, red guard WotDL

## Forest Ridge

Western Ringing Mountains spine; where most halflings come from.

- **Classes:** Druid, ranger
- **Skills:** Knowledge (nature), Survival
- **Feats:** Cannibalism Ritual\*, Jungle Fighter\*, Nature's Child\*
- **Prestige classes:** Elite sniper APXI, grove master\*, halfling protector APXI, tribal psionicist APXII

## Gulg

Predominantly human city-state inside southern Crescent Forest, east of [Tyr](../world/settlements/tyr.md).

- **Classes:** Gladiator\*, templar\*, ranger
- **Skills:** Knowledge (nature), Survival
- **Feats:** Jungle Fighter\*, Nature's Child\*
- **Prestige classes:** Ambofari APXII, dune trader\*, hunter noble APXII, elite judaga APXII, master scout APXI, Oba's servant APXI

## Jagged Cliffs

Isolated region; home to rhul-thaun (rhulisti descendants, halfling relatives) and life-shaping arts.

- **Classes:** Fighter, ranger
- **Skills:** Climb, Craft (life-shaped) LSH, Knowledge (life-shaping) LSH
- **Feats:** Cliff Combat LSH, Vertical Orientation LSH
- **Prestige classes:** Cliffclimber LSH, life-shaper LSH, windrider LSH

## Kurn

Fertile valley hidden in the White Mountains; sophisticated, cultured, mostly human residents.

- **Classes:** Cleric (air), templar\*, wizard
- **Skills:** Bluff, Knowledge (arcana)
- **Feats:** Companion\*
- **Prestige classes:** Dune trader\*, double templar WotDL, Kurnan maker FFN, Kurnan spymaster FFN, loremaster

## Nibenay

East of Tyr at northern tip of Crescent Forest; famous artisans and musicians.

- **Classes:** Bard, gladiator\*, templar\*
- **Skills:** Craft (any), Knowledge (nature), Perform (any)
- **Feats:** Artisan\*, Astrologer\*, Performance Artist\*
- **Prestige classes:** Dune trader\*, mystic dancer APXII, soulknife APXI, wife of Nibenay APXI

## Raam

East of [Urik](../world/settlements/urik.md); one of largest, most chaotic cities; very mixed population.

- **Classes:** Gladiator\*, templar\*, psion XPH
- **Skills:** Craft (any), Intimidate
- **Feats:** Artisan, Mansabbar\*, Tarandan Method\*
- **Prestige classes:** Dune trader\*, kuotagha APXI, servant of Badna APXI, psiologist\*

## Saragar

Home to the Last Sea; legendary region where the Green Age still exists. Mostly human and elves; psionics is everyday life.

- **Classes:** Druid, psion XPH, psychic warrior XPH, wilder XPH
- **Skills:** Autohypnosis XPH, Knowledge (psionics) XPH
- **Feats:** Psionic Schooling\*
- **Prestige classes:** Metamind XPH, psion uncarnate XPH

## Sea of Silt

Endless pearly plain; aggressive primitive giants. Humanoids use it for transport in specialized vehicles.

- **Classes:** Barbarian, cleric (silt), ranger
- **Skills:** Knowledge (nature), Survival
- **Feats:** Giant Killer\*
- **Prestige classes:** Elementalist\*, master shipfloater APXII

## Trembling Plains

Named for mekillot herds stampeding in early Fruitbirth, shaking the ground. Home to humans, dwarves, and half-elves known as Eloy.

- **Classes:** Fighter, ranger
- **Skills:** Survival
- **Feats:** Elfish Eloy FFN, Longshanks FFN, Wind Racer FFN
- **Prestige classes:** Wind walker FFN

## Tyr

Fertile Ringing Mountains valley; first city-state to revolt against its sorcerer-king and unban preserving magic.

- **Classes:** Gladiator\*, wizard, templar\*
- **Skills:** Craft (any), Diplomacy
- **Feats:** Companion\*, Freedom\*, Metalsmith\*
- **Prestige classes:** Black cassock APXI, draqoman APXI, dune trader\*, templar knight\*

## Urik

Northeast of Tyr between Dragon's Bowl and Smoking Crown; city of fighters (as Draj is to barbarians and cerulean wizards).

- **Classes:** Fighter, gladiator\*, psychic warrior XPH, templar\*
- **Skills:** Concentration, Craft (any), Knowledge (warcraft)\*
- **Feats:** Artisan, Disciplined
- **Prestige classes:** Dune trader\*, templar knight\*, war mind XPH, yellow robe APXI
