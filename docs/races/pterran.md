# Pterran

> "The people of the Tablelands know nothing of life. They choose no Path for themselves, and consume everything until they are dead."
> — Keltruch, pterran ranger

## Overview

Pterrans are rarely seen in the Tablelands. They live in the Hinterlands, rarely leaving village safety. Recent earthquake and storms have disrupted pterran life; more now venture outside and come to the Tyr region for trade and information.

## Personality

Among strangers, pterrans seem subdued and cautious. Once trust is earned, they are open, friendly, inquisitive, and optimistic. Personality is largely shaped by chosen **life path:**

- **Warrior:** Less disturbed by Tablelands brutality; constantly examine terrain for defense; greatest satisfaction from victory without friendly casualties.
- **Druid:** Most interested in plants, animals, and land; greatest satisfaction eliminating threats to nature.
- **Mind (psion):** Most interested in befriending and understanding individuals and societies; greatest satisfaction from intellectual accomplishments — solving mysteries, exposing deception, resolving quarrels, establishing trade routes.

## Physical description

5 to 6½ feet tall reptiles with light brown scaly skin, sharp teeth, short tail. Little clothing — belts, loincloths, sashes. Walk upright with opposing thumbs and three-fingered talon-clawed hands. Two shoulder stumps (remnants of ancient wings); finlike growth at back of head. 180–220 pounds. No visible male/female distinction.

## Relations

New to the Tablelands, unaccustomed to regional cultures. They have learned not to judge too quickly. Faith in the Earth Mother means open-minded adventure, but subdued and guarded around those not trusted. Respect for Earth Mother governs all behavior. Creatures that destroy land or show disrespect for waste creatures are regarded suspiciously. Pterrans understand natural life and death cycles but struggle with cramped living, piled refuse, and unwashed humanoid smells.

## Alignment

Tend toward lawful, well-structured lives; most are good. Evil pterran adventurers are usually outcasts who committed horrible offenses.

## Lands

Most adventuring pterrans come from [Pterran Vale](../world/settlements/pterran-vale.md) or [Lost Scale](../world/settlements/lost-scale.md) in the Hinterlands, southwest of the Tyr region.

## Magic

Wizardry's use of the environment as power source conflicts with pterran religious beliefs. Pterrans cautiously tolerate preserving magic from other races if the difference is explained.

## Psionics

Virtually all pterrans have telepathic talent; pterran psions are nearly universally telepaths. Telepathy is one of the honored life paths.

## Religion

Worship the **Earth Mother** — representation of the whole world of Athas. Devotion is deeply rooted in all culture and defines behavior. All rituals and religious events relate to Earth Mother worship — festivals honoring hunts or storm protection, priest presiding. Most pterran priests are druids.

## Language

Speak **Saurian** with an accent difficult for other races. The appendage at the back of the head creates resonant low sounds no other Tablelands race reproduces. Learn Common easily but speak it with a slight odd accent.

## Names

First name earned just after hatching, based on weather and season. After choosing a Life Path and completing apprenticeship, a title becomes the first part of the name — marks transition into pterran society.

- **Male:** Airson, Darksun, Earthsong, Suntail, Goldeye, Onesight, Terrorclaw
- **Female:** Cloudrider, Greenscale, Lifehearth, Rainkeeper, Spiritally, Watertender
- **Path names:** Aandu, Caril, Dsar, Everin, Illik, Myril, Odten, Qwes, Pex, Ptellac, Ristu, Ssrui, Tilla, Xandu
- **Villages:** Pterran Vale, Lost Scale

## Adventurers

Believe recent earthquake and disturbing events are signs from Earth Mother to become more involved in the planet's affairs — nature's upheavals mean she needs help. Brave pterrans establish contact with [Tyr](../world/settlements/tyr.md) and merchant houses for expanded contacts and information.

## Society

Based on ceremony and celebration. Central village area reserved for ceremonies. Pterrans revere Athas as Earth Mother and believe themselves her favored children. Daily thanks-giving ceremonies led by druids. Villages are collections of family dwellings. Young always born in pairs. At age 15 each pterran chooses a life path — warrior, druid, or psionicist are main paths; lesser paths exist. More follow warrior than other paths — protectors and weapon makers. Druids lead daily Earth Mother ceremonies. Fewer choose psionicist; psionics viewed as outside nature, psionicists viewed with suspicion though valued as negotiators with outsiders. Omnivores — diet from hunting and crops. Kirre, id fiend, and flailer are delicacies.

## Roleplaying suggestions

Your character class is your life path — present yourself first as druid, warrior, or psion. Daily celebrations and thanks to Earth Mother; usually find reason to be grateful. Disrespect for the land angers you. Celebrate with song and dance; humor does not extend to defiling blasphemy. Initially unfamiliar with Tyr Region customs but not primitive — observe differences with curiosity and willingness to learn unless custom harms Earth Mother or her works.

## Racial traits

- **−2 Dexterity, +2 Wisdom, +2 Charisma:** Strong confidence and keen instincts for others' motives; powerful telepaths on the mind path.
- **Humanoid (psionic, reptilian).**
- **Medium:** No special size bonuses or penalties.
- **Speed:** Base land speed 30 feet.
- **Poor hearing:** Slit ears; −2 penalty on Listen checks.
- **Natural weaponry:** Primary claw rake 1d3 each; bite 1d4 secondary (see MM natural weapons).
- **Psi-like ability:** At will — *missive* (telepathic communication with fellow reptiles only). Manifester level ½ HD (minimum 1st).
- **Weapon familiarity:** Thanak treated as martial, not exotic.
- **Life path:** Determines favored class at coming of age (cannot change after character creation):
  - Path of the Druid → druid
  - Path of the Mind → psion
  - Path of the Warrior → ranger
- **Automatic languages:** Saurian.
- **Bonus languages:** Common, Dwarven, Elven, Halfling, Giant, Gith, Kreen, Yuan-ti.
- **Favored class:** Life path (see above).
