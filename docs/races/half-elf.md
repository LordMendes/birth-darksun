# Half-elf

> "People are no good. You can only trust animals and the bottle."
> — Delmao, half-Elven thief

## Overview

Unlike the parents of muls, elves and humans are often attracted to each other. Half-elves are typically the unwanted product of a casual interracial encounter.

## Personality

Half-elves are notorious loners. Many Athasians believe half-elves combine the worst traits of both races, but the most difficult aspect — lack of self-confidence — comes not from mixed origins but from a life of rejection by both parent races. Half-elves try in vain to gain respect from humans or elves.

## Physical description

Averaging over six feet tall, half-elves combine elven dexterity with human resilience. Bulkier than elves, most find it easier to pass as full humans than full elves, but all have features hinting at elven heritage.

## Relations

Humans distrust the half-elf's elven nature; elves have no use for mixed-blood children — traditions demand such children be left behind. Human society gives half-elves a better chance of survival but even less kindness. Half-elves sometimes find friendship among muls or thri-kreen. They cooperate with companions when necessary but find it difficult to rely on anyone. Many turn to the animal world, training creatures as servants and friends. Survival skills and animal affinity developed for isolation make them valuable beast handlers in human society.

## Alignment

Lawful and neutral half-elves labor for acceptance from a parent race; chaotic ones have given up on acceptance and reject the society that rejected them.

## Lands

Half-elves do not form communities. Those who settle down tend to live among humans, who at least find a use for them.

## Magic

Half-elves often take up arcane studies — a solitary calling.

## Psionics

Mastery of the Way often provides independence and self-knowledge half-elves seek; psionic academy membership can provide acceptance.

## Religion

Alienation from society and affinity with animals make half-elves excellent druids. Some turn resentment into sullen, bullying templar careers. As clerics they are drawn to water's healing influence.

## Language

Half-elves speak Common. A few learn Elven.

## Names

Half-elves nearly always have human names. Unable to run as elves, they never receive elven given names or tribal surnames.

## Adventurers

In a party, half-elves often seem detached and aloof.

## Society

Half-elves do not consider themselves a separate race and rarely form half-elven communities. Life is typically harder than for humans or elves. Elves have no tolerance for mixed heritage; humans do not trust the elvish side. Humans are far more tolerant than elves, who often refuse mixed children into tribes and may cast the mother out. Most half-elves are outsiders who wander their entire lives. They develop high self-reliance — pride that can seem aloof. Many turn to animals for company.

## Roleplaying suggestions

Desperate for approval from elves or humans, you are even more desperate to appear independent and self-reliant to cover that desire. You tend toward feisty, insecure, sullen self-reliance, refusing favors. You show off skills in front of elves and humans but react awkwardly or suspiciously to praise. Closest childhood friendships were with animals. Other half-elves do not interest you. Over time you may get along with dwarves, pterrans, muls, even thri-kreen — you do not need their approval, yet they give it more readily.

## Racial traits

- **+2 Dexterity, −2 Charisma:** Limber like elven parents; upbringing leaves poor self-image affecting relations.
- **Humanoid (elf).**
- **Medium:** No size bonuses or penalties.
- **Speed:** Base land speed 30 feet.
- **Low-light vision** (as Player's Handbook).
- **+2 racial bonus on Disguise checks** when impersonating elves or humans.
- **+1 racial bonus** on Listen, Search, and Spot checks.
- **+2 racial bonus** on Survival and Handle Animal checks.
- **Elven blood:** For all effects related to race, considered an elf (vulnerable to elf-affecting effects; may use elf-only magic items).
- **Automatic languages:** Common and Elven.
- **Bonus languages:** Any.
- **Favored class:** Any (highest-level class does not count when determining multiclass XP penalty).
