# Other races

Athas is a place where members of different races are usually found in the same city-state — often as gladiatorial exotic attractions or slaves valued for physical might. Even when not concentrated in one area, these races are significant players in the Tablelands.

Belgoi, gith, jozhals, ssurrans, tareks, taris, yuan-ti, and a variety of other creatures commonly viewed as monsters might appear as player characters in a Dark Sun campaign.

## Where to find rules

All rules needed to play one of these races are in:

- *Terrors of Athas* (Athas.org)
- *Expanded Psionics Handbook*
- *Dungeon Master's Guide*

Cultural information about several monstrous races appears in DS3 v8 Chapter 7: Life on Athas.
