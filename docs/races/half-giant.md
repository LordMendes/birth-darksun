# Half-giant

> "Mind of a child, strength of three grown men. I've seen a half-giant tear the walls out of a building because he wanted a better look at the tattoos on a mul inside."
> — Daro, human trader

## Overview

Legend holds that a sorcerer-queen used wizardry to beget a union of giant and human to create powerful slaves. Whatever the truth, half-giants have increased in number and are fairly common especially in human-controlled lands near the Sea of Silt. They gain great strength but dull wits from giant heritage, and are nearly as agile as their human forbears.

## Personality

Because of artificial origins, there is no half-giant culture, tradition, or homeland. Half-giants readily imitate neighbors' customs and cultures. They often display curiosity, willingness to learn, and general kindness.

## Physical description

Enormous — about 11½ feet tall and around 1,200 pounds. Thick hair, often braided (especially females) or in a single tail down the back. Garb suits occupation or environment. Mature at about 24 years; live about 170 years.

## Relations

The most powerful warriors on Athas, half-giants seem content in humanity's shadow. Friendly and eager to please, they adopt lifestyles, skills, and values of those they admire. Encountering a new situation, a half-giant watches what others do — e.g. at a dwarven quarry he may start quarrying stone; if he can make a living at it he continues like his dwarf neighbors, otherwise he moves on.

## Alignment

Half-giants switch attitudes quickly, taking new values to fit new situations. A peaceful farmer disrupted by marauders may adopt the renegades' morals. A half-giant's nature is to switch alignment aspect to imitate or react to significant change. **Axis alignment:** One alignment aspect is fixed at character creation; the other is chosen each morning upon waking and lasts until sleep. Alignment change is not mandatory.

## Lands

Most often found in city-states as gladiators, laborers, soldiers, and guards. A few wilderness communities adopt neighboring cultures. Rare half-giant communities attach to a charismatic leader (not necessarily half-giant) who demonstrates admired tendencies.

## Magic

If companions accept wizardry, the half-giant accepts it. If companions hate wizardry, the half-giant joins in stoning a wizard. Among companions who accept preserving but despise defiling magic, all but the brightest half-giants become confused and look to companions for how to react.

## Psionics

Single-classed half-giant psions are very rare. Some take psychic warrior, becoming killing machines that can take apart a mekillot barehanded.

## Religion

No affinity for worship of one element over another.

## Language

All half-giants speak Common (slave speech). Whatever tongue they speak, their voice is pitched so low as to occasionally be difficult to understand.

## Names

Enslaved half-giants often have human names (vary greatly). Free half-giants borrow naming conventions of the race or people they imitate when their child is born.

## Adventurers

Usually led to adventure by interesting companions of other races.

## Society

A relatively young race with little cultural identity — they adopt customs and beliefs of cultures they live among and routinely change alignment to match influential neighbors. Found across the Tablelands, congregating near population centers. Rarely form own communities. Unlike some bastard races, half-giants **can reproduce** — one offspring per union after almost a year of pregnancy. Omnivorous but require **twice** the food and water of humans. Clothing and equipment need twice the material (higher prices). They damage objects and buildings through size alone. Some camp outside city walls to limit damage; draw of city culture and below-average intellect limits how many do so.

## Roleplaying suggestions

Remember how much bigger and heavier you are. Take advantage of height in combat but remember disadvantages. Between size and lesser wits (even intelligent half-giants are assumed dull), you are comic relief. You endure teasing until pushed too far — then personality can shift and unleash astonishing violence. Shifts can also happen without provocation — wake with different ethos and disposition. You are influenced by powerful personalities; imitate tactics, clothes, and demeanor of your "little master."

## Racial traits

- **+8 Strength, +4 Constitution, −2 Dexterity, −4 Intelligence, −4 Wisdom, −4 Charisma:** Renowned for great strength and dull wits.
- **Large:** −1 size penalty to AC and attack rolls; reach 10 feet.
- **Giant type.**
- **Speed:** Base land speed 40 feet.
- **Darkvision** 60 feet (black and white only).
- **Natural armor +2** to AC.
- **Axis alignment:** One alignment aspect fixed at creation; other chosen each morning (e.g. fixed lawful → each morning choose lawful good, lawful neutral, or lawful evil until sleep). Change not mandatory.
- **Automatic languages:** Common.
- **Bonus languages:** Dwarven, Gith, Giant (often pick up a race's tongue when imitating them long enough).
- **Favored class:** Barbarian (does not count when determining multiclass XP penalty).
- **Level adjustment +2.**
