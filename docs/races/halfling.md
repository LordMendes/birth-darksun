# Halfling

> "Be wary of the forest ridge. The halflings who live there would as soon eat you alive as look at you. Chances are you won't even notice them until you've become the main course."
> — Mo'rune, half-Elven ranger

## Overview

Halflings are masters of the jungles of the Ringing Mountains — small, quick, agile creatures steeped in ancient culture going back far into Athas's past. Though uncommon in the Tablelands, some leave the forests to adventure under the Dark Sun. As carnivores, halflings prefer flesh raw.

## Personality

Halflings have difficulty understanding others' customs or points of view; curiosity helps some overcome xenophobia. Little concerned with material wealth; more concerned with how actions affect other halflings.

## Physical description

About 3½ feet tall, 50–60 pounds. Rarely affected by age — faces often mistaken for human children. Loincloths, sometimes shirt or vest; bright red and green body paint. Forest halflings rarely tend hair; some let it grow long, unkempt, and dirty. Live about 120 years.

## Relations

Culture dominates relations with others. Halflings relate well to each other through shared cultural traits. Different tribes share song, art, and poetry as communication basis. Creatures unfamiliar with these expressions are at a loss; halflings grow frustrated with "uncultured" beings. They abhor slavery; most starve rather than accept it.

## Alignment

Tend toward law and evil. Uncomfortable with change; rely on racial identity, family, clan ties, and personal honor. Little respect for big people's laws.

## Lands

Halfling villages are rare in the Tablelands. Most live in tribes or clans in the Forest Ridge or Rohorind forest west of [Kurn](../world/settlements/kurn.md). Many dwell in treetop villages. Non-halflings typically see these villages only from inside a halfling cooking pot.

## Magic

Many tribes reject arcane magic. Tribes that accept wizards tend to have preserver chieftains. Only renegade tribes harbor defilers.

## Psionics

Many become seers or nomads. In the Forest Ridge, tribal halflings often multiclass seer/ranger — among the deadliest trackers on Athas.

## Religion

Bond with nature extends through culture. Shaman or witch doctor often rules clans as spiritual leader, obeyed without question. Fighters willingly sacrifice themselves for the leader.

## Language

Halflings rarely teach their language; some Tablelands individuals have learned the wild speech. Tablelands halflings often learn Common.

## Names

Usually one given name.

- **Male:** Basha, Cerk, Derlan, Drassu, Entrok, Kakzim, Lokee, Nok, Pauk, Plool, Sala, Tanuka, Ukos, Zol
- **Female:** Alansa, Anezka, Dokala, Grelzen, Horga, Jikx, Joura, Nasaha, Vensa

## Adventurers

Exploring the Tablelands lets curious halflings learn other customs. Natural curiosity enables interaction despite initial difficulty. Others are criminals, renegades, or outcasts escaping persecution.

## Society

Common outlook produces racial unity across tribal ties. Rarely will one halfling draw another's blood even in extreme disagreement — only renegades lack this unity and are cast out. Society is difficult for other races: conquest and plundering have no place. Most important value is the inner self harmonizing with environment and halfling race. Extremely conscious of environment — sickened by the ruined Tyr region, desperate to prevent similar devastation in the Forest Ridge. Culture expressed through art and song; oral history passed to next generation. Shared culture expresses abstract thought — halflings assume listeners share halfling history and find it hard to compensate for those who "lack culture." Wandering halflings are curious about outside societies but **never** adopt other cultures, believing halfling culture innately superior. They do not seek to change others' cultures. Omnivorous but vastly prefer meat — view living creatures as food more than equals, and believe other races see them the same way; rarely trust other races.

## Roleplaying suggestions

Consistently account for height. Role-play halfling culture: eating opponents, trust and kindness to fellow halflings, suspicion of big people, general lack of interest in money.

## Racial traits

- **−2 Strength, +2 Dexterity:** Quick and stealthy but weaker than humans.
- **−2 penalty on all Diplomacy checks** when dealing with other races.
- **Small:** +1 size bonus to AC and attack rolls.
- **Speed:** Base land speed 20 feet.
- **+2 racial bonus** on Climb, Jump, and Move Silently checks.
- **+2 racial bonus on saving throws** against spells and spell-like effects.
- **+1 racial attack bonus** with thrown weapons (javelins and slings common in feral halfling society).
- **+4 racial bonus on Listen checks;** equally keen smell and taste — +4 on Wisdom checks assessing smell or taste.
- **Automatic languages:** Halfling.
- **Bonus languages:** Common, Dwarven, Elven, Gith, Kreen, Rhul-thaun, Sylvan, Yuan-ti.
- **Favored class:** Ranger (does not count when determining multiclass XP penalty).
