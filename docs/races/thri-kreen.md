# Thri-kreen

> "This one does not speak with the quivering soft shells that lay about all night. This one might eat you, but never speak."
> — Tu'tochuk

## Overview

Thri-kreen are the strangest intelligent races of the Tablelands — insectoid beings with a mindset very different from any humanoid. They roam the wastes in packs, hunting day and night (they require no sleep). Quick, agile, and fearsome fighters, feared throughout the wastes.

## Personality

Since kreen do not require sleep, they have difficulty understanding this need in humanoids — a state of "laziness." Other humanoid behaviors seem unnecessarily complex. A kreen's life is simple: hunt prey. Kreen live for the hunt and own only what they can carry.

## Physical description

Mature kreen stand about 7 feet tall with rough body length of 11 feet. Four clawed arms; two powerful legs capable of incredible leaps (unable to jump backwards). Sandy-yellow chitin exoskeleton. Head topped with two antennae; compound multifaceted eyes; mouth of small pincers. Males and females physically indistinguishable. Usually no clothing — harness for weapons and food. Leg or armbands, bracelets; some attach rings to chitin (requires skilled artisan).

## Relations

Pack mentality dominates relations. Kreen hunt in packs, moving on before prey is depleted. A kreen joining humanoids often tries to establish dominance — threatening gestures that unsettle the unaware. Once settled, they abide by the outcome. View humanoids as food sources but usually hunt them only in dire need. Many have fond taste for elves — meetings with elves are often tense. Once part of a clutch, thri-kreen never turn on humanoid friends even in worst situations.

## Alignment

Most are lawful — pack mentality is ingrained. Kreen deviating from pack mentality are rare.

## Lands

No thri-kreen settlements in the Tyr region; kreen there are small packs or adventuring with humanoids. North of the Tyr region, beyond the Jagged Cliffs and Misty Border, lies the **Kreen Empire** — great kreen nation ruling the Crimson Savanna with city-states rivaling humanoid city-states.

## Magic

No natural disposition toward magic; wizardry's use of environment conflicts with kreen beliefs. Lack of sleep and instinctual need to hunt do not suit magical study. Kreen wizards are extremely rare — none seen in the Tablelands.

## Psionics

Psionics are natural. Some packs use telepathy to coordinate hunting. Many augment combat with psychometabolic powers (speed, metabolism, strength). Most kreen (even non-adventurers) take psychic warrior — considered natural growing up. Kreen do not need instruction to advance in psychic warrior; it comes from ancestral memory.

## Religion

No devotion to any god; nature and elements held in high regard. Ancestral memories guide lives. Revere the **Great One**, legendary kreen leader. Language of clicks, pops, grinding noises — no lips or tongues.

## Language

Kreen language differs greatly from other intelligent races.

## Names

Kachka, Ka'Cha, Ka'Ka'Kyl, Klik-Chaka'da, Sa'Relka, T'Chai

## Adventurers

Most enjoy challenges of new prey. Some seek leadership of new clutches and observe different "hunting" techniques of the *dra* (sentient meat-creatures such as humans).

## Society

Hatch from eggs; same-hatchlings form a **clutch**. Gather in **packs** of several clutches roaming hunting territory. No permanent communities. Strict dominance order — toughest leads; challenge by contest until surrender or death; matter settled without lingering resentment (challenger acted to strengthen pack). Obsessed with hunting; carnivores but seldom hunt intelligent life for food (except taste for elf). When not hunting: craft weapons, teach young, sculpt. Pack mentality applied to every situation — compelled to be part of a clutch; accept other races as clutch-mates.

## Roleplaying suggestions

Rely on natural attacks and kreen weapons. Everything killed is potential dinner. Strong need for party leader — obedience important. If you seem most capable, assume leadership; if challenged, test who is stronger (not to the death). No dwarf-like focus, but would give life to protect companions. If you did not trust them as family you would not travel together. Do not understand sleep — disturbs you that dra companions lie unconscious a third of their lives. Own only what you carry; little care for money or treasure. Philosophy of ownership sometimes conflicts with dra who think they own land, buildings, herds.

## Racial traits

- **+2 Strength, +4 Dexterity, −2 Intelligence, +2 Wisdom, −4 Charisma:** Fast; alien mindset hinders relating to humanoids; clutch-mind leaves poor sense of self.
- **Monstrous Humanoid:** Not subject to humanoid-only spells/effects.
- **Medium:** No size bonuses or penalties.
- **Speed:** Base land speed 40 feet.
- **Darkvision** 60 feet.
- **Sleep immunity:** Do not sleep; immune to sleep spells and similar effects. Spellcasters/manifesters still require 8 hours rest before preparing spells.
- **Natural armor +2** (chitin).
- **Multiple limbs:** Four arms — may take Multiweapon Fighting (MM 304) instead of Two-Weapon Fighting; may take Multiattack (not bonus feats).
- **Natural weapons:** Full-round claw attacks — primary claws 1d4 each (four claws); secondary bite 1d4 with poison chance. May attack with weapon(s) at normal bonus plus bite or claw as secondary.
- **Leap:** +30 racial bonus on Jump checks.
- **Deflect Arrows:** Gain Deflect Arrows feat.
- **Poison (Ex):** Bite delivers poison (Fort DC 11 + Con modifier); initial 1d6 Dex damage, secondary paralysis. One bite's poison per day.
- **Weapon familiarity:** Chatkcha and gythka treated as martial, not exotic.
- **+4 racial bonus on Hide checks** in sandy or arid areas.
- **Automatic languages:** Kreen.
- **Bonus languages:** Common, Dwarven, Elven, Entomic, Saurian, Terran.
- **Favored class:** Psychic warrior.
- **Level adjustment +2.**
