# Character races

Athas is a world of many races — gith in the deserts, tareks too stubborn to know when they have died, giants in the Silt Sea, belgoi in the night. The Pristine Tower's magic produces New Races that rarely see a second generation. Despite that variety, only a few races have the numbers and motivation to shape life in the Tablelands.

Though the races of Dark Sun resemble those of other campaign worlds, it is frequently in name only. Insular elves roam the wastes, trusted by no one but their tribe-mates. Halflings are feral creatures with a taste for human flesh. Hairless dwarves work endlessly, their world filtered through a single all-consuming task. Unsleeping thri-kreen roam the wastes, always hunting their next meal.

**Table rule:** Apply only the benefits and penalties described in DS3 v8 for each race. Do not stack bonuses from other sources unless DS3 explicitly says so.
