# Vital statistics

Age, gender, height, weight, and appearance are player choices unless you prefer DS3 guidelines below. These tables replace Player's Handbook p. 109 for this campaign.

## Random starting ages

Adulthood plus class-based modifiers (Table 1–2).

| Race | Adulthood | Barbarian, rogue | Bard, fighter, gladiator, psychic warrior, ranger | Cleric, druid, psion, templar, wizard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human | 15 years | +1d4 | +1d6 | +2d6 |
| Aarakocra | 8 years | +1d4 | +1d6 | +2d4 |
| Dwarf | 30 years | +2d6 | +4d6 | +6d6 |
| Elf | 20 years | +1d4 | +1d6 | +2d6 |
| Half-elf | 15 years | +1d6 | +2d6 | +3d6 |
| Half-giant | 25 years | +1d6 | +2d6 | +4d6 |
| Halfling | 20 years | +3d6 | +3d6 | +4d6 |
| Mul | 14 years | +1d4 | +1d6 | +2d6 |
| Pterran | 10 years | +1d6 | +1d6 | +1d6 |
| Thri-kreen | 4 years | +1d4 | +1d4 | +1d4 |

As characters age, physical ability scores change per Player's Handbook p. 109.
