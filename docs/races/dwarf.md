# Dwarf

> "The worst thing you can say to a dwarf is 'It can't be done.' If he's already decided to do it, he may never speak to you again. If he hasn't decided to take up the task, he may commit himself to it simply out of spite. 'Impossible' is not a concept most dwarves understand. Anything can be done, with enough determination."
> — Sha'len, Nibenese trader

## Overview

Dwarves form a good part of the people encountered in the Tablelands. These strong, devoted beings live to fulfill their **focus** — a task they choose to devote their lives to. Stubborn and strong-minded, dwarves make good companions, though their focused nature can be bothersome.

## Personality

Dwarves prefer meaningful tasks and approach them with intensity rarely seen in other races. They make excellent laborers and take great pride in accomplishments. Their stubbornness leads to difficulties — dwarves sometimes fail to listen to reason, attempting impossible tasks. Dwarves live for their focus. Dwarves who die unable to complete their focus return from the dead as banshees to haunt unfinished work. A dwarf rarely divulges his focus to anyone.

## Physical description

Tablelands dwarves stand 4½ to 5 feet tall with big muscular limbs and a strong build, averaging 200 lbs. Dwarves are **hairless** and find the very idea of hair repulsive. They have deeply tanned skin and rarely decorate it with tattoos. Dwarves can live up to 250 years.

## Relations

A dwarf's relations with others often depend on his focus. Those who help or share his goals are treated with respect. There is little room for compromise with those who disagree or hinder the focus — they are obstacles to be removed. Community is important; dwarves have strong racial affinity. They rarely share history with non-dwarves; it can take years for a stranger to gain trust enough for admission into a dwarven family circle.

## Alignment

Dwarves tend toward lawful alignment, mostly good or neutral. Devotion to village hierarchy means they follow rules, sometimes to the point of ridicule.

## Lands

Three main dwarven settlements in the Tablelands: **Kled**, near [Tyr](../world/settlements/tyr.md); and the twin villages [North Ledopolus](../world/settlements/north-ledopolus.md) and [South Ledopolus](../world/settlements/south-ledopolus.md) on the southwestern edge of the Tablelands. Some communities exist in city-states and small villages; others live with slave tribes of the wastes.

## Magic

Like most peoples, dwarves have an aversion to wizardly magic and are least amenable to changing their minds. Dwarves rarely take to the wizardly arts; those who do are usually shunned. Some dwarves travel with a wizard who proves himself a worthy companion, but few dwarves truly trust a wizard.

## Psionics

Dwarves take to psionics with a vengeance. They make formidable egoists and nomads.

## Religion

Dwarven communities are ruled by elders; dwarves are devoted to the community leader, the **Urhnomous**. Dwarves typically worship elemental earth. Fire is sometimes worshiped for destructive power; water for healing. Air's intangibility and chaotic nature attract few dwarven worshippers. Dwarven druids are unusual and tend to guard particular lands.

## Language

Dwarves have a long, proud oral history and an old written language used mostly for histories. They will not teach their ancient language to outsiders. Dwarven is deep and throaty — guttural sounds and harsh exclamations. Most non-dwarves get raw throats speaking it for more than a few hours.

## Names

A dwarf's name is usually granted by the clan leader after completing his first focus.

- **Male:** Baranus, Biirgaz, Bontar, Brul, Caelum, Caro, Daled, Drog, Fyra, Ghedran, Gralth, Gram, Jurgan, Lyanius, Murd, Nati, Portek
- **Female:** Ardin, Erda, Ghava, Greshin, Gudak, Lazra, N'kadir, Palashi, Vashara

## Adventurers

Dwarves adventure to learn about the Tablelands (young and brash), to complete a focus that takes them from their communities, or to search for ancient dwarven villages and their treasures.

## Society

No dwarf is more content than while working toward a focus. A focus must take at least a week to complete (8 hours minimum each day); anything less is too simple. Free dwarves form clan-based communities focused on family. Blood ties are honored above all except the focus. Family honor passes across generations. Community leaders are **Urhnomous** (over-leader); each clan is led by an **uhrnius**. Most free dwarves earn through trade — metal smiths and mercenaries stand out. Athasians acknowledge dwarven-forged metal among the best. Some dwarves scavenge steel scraps for smiths.

## Roleplaying suggestions

Remember the intensity of your focus. Breaking or ignoring a focus has social, philosophical, and spiritual repercussions. For someone to stand in your way is an assault. There is no greater satisfaction than fulfilling a difficult focus. Keep a serious, sober attitude nearly always. The only time you show festive side is after recently fulfilling a focus, during the hours or days until you set a new one — and only to other dwarves and most trusted non-dwarven friends. Those days are also vulnerability: until you set a new focus you lose all focus-related bonuses.

## Racial traits

- **+2 Constitution, −2 Charisma:** Strong and sturdy, but single-mindedness hinders dealings with others.
- **Humanoid (dwarf).**
- **Medium:** No special size bonuses or penalties.
- **Darkvision** 60 feet (black and white only).
- **Speed:** Base land speed 20 feet; can move this speed in medium or heavy armor or when carrying medium or heavy load.
- **Stability:** +4 bonus on ability checks to resist bull rush or trip when standing on the ground.
- **+2 racial bonus on saving throws** against poison.
- **Weapon familiarity:** Urgrosh treated as martial, not exotic.
- **+2 racial bonus on saving throws** against spells and spell-like effects.
- **Focus:** +1 morale bonus on all checks directly related to the focus (skill, attack, damage, save, manifestation, or spell save DC). Focus must require at least five days (8 hours minimum each day) to complete; DM determines when the bonus applies. Must finish one focus before choosing another. Dwarves who die before finishing may rise as banshees.
- **Automatic languages:** Common and Dwarven.
- **Bonus languages:** Elven, Giant, Gith, Kreen, Saurian.
- **Favored class:** Fighter (does not count when determining multiclass XP penalty).
