# Elf

> "Honor? The word does not exist in the Elven language."
> — Tharak, human guard

## Overview

Athas's deserts, plains, steppes, and badlands are home to the elves — a long-limbed race of trading, raiding, thieving sprinters. **Running** is the key to acceptance and respect among elves. Elves injured and unable to run are often left behind to die.

## Personality

Other races see elves as dishonest and lazy — generally a fair assessment. Elves idle for days until need compels them, but can run for days without complaint. No self-respecting elf will consent to ride an animal; to do so is dishonorable. Elven custom dictates individuals keep up or be left behind. Elves prefer short, happy lives over long, boring ones. Seeing the future as dark and deadly, they live in the now. They thrive in open spaces and wither in captivity.

## Physical description

Elves stand between 6 and 7 feet tall with lean builds, angular deeply etched features, and no facial hair. They dress in garb designed to protect from desert and elements.

## Relations

Elves keep to their own tribe and proven friends unless they have an angle — something to sell or some deception to pass off. Strangers are potential enemies; elves look for every opportunity to win advantage. If an elf believes a companion might be worthy, he devises "tests of trust" to prove friendship is "stronger than the bonds of death." Once trust is gained, the stranger is forever that elf's friend. Betrayal ends trust forever.

## Alignment

Elves tend toward chaos for love of freedom, variety, and self-expression. With respect to good and evil they tend toward neutrality, though behavior leans good — even self-sacrifice — where the tribe's good is at stake. They steal freely but are not murderous; they rarely attack except those who threaten them or stand in their way.

## Lands

Always at home running in the wastes, elves act as if all plains and badlands were elven lands. Since most elves are loath to settle or build, they can rarely enforce claims. Tribes make a living through herding, raiding, or trading; most have plied all three. A tribe's current occupation determines lands claimed — herders claim grazing lands, raiders claim trade-route lands, traders claim no lands but wander for bargains and loose purses.

## Magic

Of all Tablelands races, elves have the greatest affinity toward and acceptance of arcane practices.

## Psionics

Persistence is not an elven strong suit, so Elven Will is often weaker than other races'. A few elves study the Way for advantage in battle and trade.

## Religion

Elves revere **Coraanu Star Racer** as the ideal First Elf — the warrior thief, embodiment of all elves wish to be. Their calendar is based on his life; his myth is honored with song, dance, and celebration. Many elves worship the elements, particularly air (freedom, swiftness, song). Elves honor and swear by the moons — low-light vision turns moonlight into an elven advantage.

## Language

Elves share a common language with distinct tribal dialects. Elven is short, clipped words at rapid staccato pace, difficult for novices. Most elves condescend to learn Common for trade. Elves who learn other tongues often hide the ability.

## Names

Whether slave or free, elves prefer Elven names. Tribe members take the tribe name as surname. Naming young runners is sacred — children are named after the first interesting thing they do while learning to run. With the appropriate name a child can grow to greatness; with the wrong name the elf may vanish in the wastes. Sometimes a name changes after an extraordinary rite-of-passage deed.

- **Female:** Alaa (Bird Chaser), Ekee (Wild Dancer), Guuta (Singing Sword), Hukaa (Fire Leaper), Ittee (Dancing Bow), Nuuta (Quiet Hunter), Utaa (Laughing Moon)

## Adventurers

Elves often adventure from wanderlust; those who persist generally seek profit, glory, revenge, or loyalty to companions who won friendship. Elves love to boast or have deeds woven into song. They hoard keepsakes from memorable raids — some quilt stolen clothing into cloaks. Little pleases elves like flaunting a stolen item before its original owner. Custom dictates the victim acknowledge the accomplishment by congratulating the thief. Those who fail to show gallantry are poor sports. Adventurers who keep tribal membership should give their chief periodic choice of treasure; holding out suggests lack of loyalty.

## Society

Intense tribal unity does not extend beyond the tribe — elves from other tribes are potential enemies. Within a tribe all elves are equal except the chief, who rules for life and makes major decisions. Chief selection varies: some elect the best leader; others inherit leadership. Elves do not huddle in conference or follow orders at length — love of freedom keeps them from court intrigues. They prefer intrigues against outsiders. Acceptance requires bravery and willingness to sacrifice; being an elf does not help. Companions face constant tests of trust until the elf accepts them or they fail. Years of conditioning give all elves ability to move quickly over sandy and rocky terrain and run long distances — elves spurn riding beasts. Elven culture is rich in song and dance — the most captivating in the Tablelands. Celebrations revolve around tribal heroes ancient and current.

## Roleplaying suggestions

Rely on distance, bows, and fighting by moon and star light. Use running and escape from entangling situations. When someone professes friendship, dismiss them at first, then offer a test of trust (without telling them it is a test). Ask for a prize possession, leave valuables out to see if they steal, pretend to sleep and listen to what they say. Some elves allow themselves to be captured to see if the presumed friend will rescue them.

## Racial traits

- **+2 Dexterity, −2 Constitution:** Agile but less resilient than humans.
- **Humanoid (elf).**
- **Medium:** No special size bonuses or penalties.
- **Speed:** Base land speed 40 feet.
- **Low-light vision:** See twice as far as a human in moonlight and similar conditions, retaining color and detail.
- **Proficient with all bows.**
- **Weapon familiarity:** Elven longblade treated as martial weapon.
- **+2 racial bonus** on Listen, Perform, Search, and Spot checks.
- **Heat endurance:** Natural resistance to extreme temperatures — treat extreme heat or cold as very hot or cold (DMG temperature rules); suffer normally from abysmal heat or magical/supernatural heat and cold.
- **Elf run:** After one minute warm-up and Concentration check (DC 10), elves enter an elf run state — hustle for long distances as easily as a human moves normally, and run for long distances as easily as a human hustles. Each continued day requires additional Concentration checks: DC 10 (day 2), DC 15 (day 3), DC 20 (day 4), DC 30 (day 5), DC 40 (day 6). Failure ends benefits and imposes normal extended movement penalties (PH p. 164). After a full day's rest, may attempt again. In a group, runners add the leader's Charisma bonus to movement rate and related Fortitude checks.
- **Automatic languages:** Common and Elven.
- **Bonus languages:** Dwarven, Entomic, Kreen, Gith, Saurian, Terran.
- **Favored class:** Rogue (does not count when determining multiclass XP penalty).
