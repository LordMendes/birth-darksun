# Legacy power descriptions

Athasian scion powers by [legacy](../legacies.md). General rules: [Scions and Legacy Score](../godshards.md). Roll on the [legacy tables](legacy-tables.md), then look up the power here.

**Encounter** = one combat. **Day** = 24 hours unless noted.

**Type:** Ex = extraordinary (works in antimagic). Su = supernatural. Sp = spell-like (SR and *dispel magic* apply).

**Jump to:** [Sun](#sun-legacy) · [Storm](#storm-legacy) · [Sea](#sea-legacy) · [Stone](#stone-legacy) · [Mind](#mind-legacy) · [Defiler](#defiler-legacy) · [Dragon](#dragon-legacy)

## Defiler legacy

Life-draining magic. Using these powers advances **local defiling** unless covenant is spent on preservation. [Legacy description](../legacies.md#defiler).

### Minor

| Power | Type | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Life Siphon | Su | Kill a living creature with an arcane spell: regain 1 hp per spell level. |
| Defiler's Sense | Su | On concentration, tell whether nearby (60 ft.) arcane casting was defiling or preserving. |
| Corrupted Magic | Ex | +1 caster level on damaging arcane spells. |
| Ashen Touch | Su | Melee touch wilts plants; 1d4 to plant creatures. 3/day. |
| Gray Sight | Su | See defiled terrain as gray haze. +4 Survival to avoid defiled zones. |

### Major

| Power | Type | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drain Life | Su | 1/day: when an arcane spell damages a living creature, heal half the damage dealt. |
| Ashen Spell | Su | 1/day: one arcane spell deals +50% damage; vegetation within 10 ft. withers. |
| Life Drain Aura | Su | 1/encounter when you cast an arcane spell: creatures within 10 ft. Fort DC 10 + ½ level + Cha or 1 Con damage. |
| Withering Gaze | Sp | Gaze 30 ft., 2d6 + 1 Str, Fort half. 3/day. |
| Defiler's Mark | Su | Visible when you cast. +2 Intimidate, −2 Diplomacy with druids and preservers. |
| Sorcerous Thirst | Su | Extra highest-level arcane slot. After rest, 30-ft. defilement. 1/day. |

### Great

| Power | Type | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Devastating Defilement | Su | 1/day: maximize one arcane spell (no slot change). Terrain defiled in 60-ft. radius. |
| Life Devourer | Su | Kill a living creature with magic: temp hp = 2 × victim HD (max 20 × your level). |
| Black Sun | Su | 1/week: 60-ft. radius, 10 minutes. Vegetation dies; living creatures fatigued (Fort each minute). Your arcane spells +2 CL inside. |
