# Legacy powers

Athasian **scions** manifest **legacy powers** — minor, major, and great abilities tied to a [primordial legacy](../legacies.md). Structure matches [Birthright blood ability tables](https://birthright.net/wiki/index.php/Blood_abilities); powers are original Athasian content.

## How many powers

Determined by [Legacy Score](../godshards.md#legacy-score-and-powers) (LS). Example: LS 18 → one minor, one major, one great.

You may choose powers of **lower** strength than entitled (e.g. three minors instead of one major).

## Legacy Score ranks

| LS | Rank |
| ---: | --- |
| 1–10 | Weak (usually no powers) |
| 11–20 | Minor |
| 21–30 | Strong |
| 31–40 | Great |
| 41+ | Legendary |

## Choosing or rolling

1. Know your **legacy** ([legacies](../legacies.md))
2. Know your **Legacy Score** and allowed tiers
3. **Choose** from [legacy tables](legacy-tables.md), or **roll** d100 on the column for the tier you are filling
4. Meet prerequisites marked * in tables
5. Read full text in [descriptions](descriptions.md)

## Hereditary powers

Families may be known for specific powers (e.g. Desert Adaptation in a Tyrian sun-cult line). Prefer those when building or rolling (~75% on random rolls).

## Prerequisites

| Power | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- |
| Call Lightning | Storm legacy; LS 24+; scion level 1 |
| Dominate Thought | Mind legacy; LS 28+; scion level 1 |
| Path to Dragonhood | Dragon legacy; LS 36+; scion level 3; DM oversight |

## Using legacy powers

See [Scions and Legacy Score](../godshards.md#using-legacy-powers). **Defiler** powers always advance local terrain decay unless covenant is spent on preservation.
