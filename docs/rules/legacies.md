# Primordial legacies

When the gods died at the [Shattering](../campaign/premise.md), their essence did not stay divine. It **fractured** into primordial **legacies** — elemental, psionic, and defiling strains soaked into mortal blood. A **scion** inherits one legacy, not a living god's portfolio.

Two things define a scion:

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Legacy** | Which ancient power runs in the blood (Sun, Storm, Sea, Stone, Mind, Defiler, Dragon) |
| **Legacy type (rank)** | How strongly that power manifests ([Legacy Score](godshards.md): Weak, Minor, Strong, Great, Legendary) |

Mechanics use Birthright-style tables: [Legacy Score](godshards.md) sets how many powers you have; [legacy tables](blood-abilities/legacy-tables.md) set which ones.

## Legacy kinds

The seven named legacies fall into four **kinds**. Kind is flavor and social reaction; it is not a second bloodline.

| Kind | Legacies | Source of the blood |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Elemental** | Sun, Storm, Sea, Stone | Green Age elemental champions; closest to the old gods of fire, air, water, and earth |
| **Psionic** | Mind | Ancient lineages of the Way; the most common scion blood on Athas |
| **Defiling** | Defiler | Sorcerer-king experiments, catastrophic defilement, or blood soaked at a source wound |
| **Draconic** | Dragon | Exposure to a Dragon's metamorphosis; extremely rare |

Elemental scions are often claimed by matching elemental cults. Mind scions are courted by nobles and academies of the Way. Defiler and Dragon scions draw templar and sorcerer-king attention.

## The seven legacies

### Sun

Fire, radiance, and endurance under the crimson sky. Sun blood is the desert made flesh: heat that does not kill its bearer, light that marks them, and a presence that templars and warriors recognize.

**Origin:** Descendants of Green Age fire-elemental champions, or of those who stood closest when the Burned Sun's essence leaked at Godshold.

**Who carries it:** Desert fighters, sun-cult templars, caravan guards, nobles who claim "the sun's favor." Common in the Tablelands; rare among underground folk.

**Mark:** Gold or amber eyes, heat-shimmer in still air, skin that does not burn. Some lines have a white-gold streak in the hair.

**On Athas:** Sun scions drink less, walk farther, and fight with fire in their blood. Great powers can turn a warrior into a walking flare. Cults of fire and the sorcerer-kings' sun-temples (Draj especially) treat a visible Sun mark as omen or property.

**Typical powers:** Heat and thirst resistance, solar weapons, desert movement, Avatar of the Sun, Sunburst.

---

### Storm

Air, lightning, and speed. Storm blood is the wind over the silt, the dust devil, the thunder that arrives before rain that never comes.

**Origin:** Air-elemental champions of the Green Age, or bloodlines that drank the shattered sky-god's remnant.

**Who carries it:** Nomads, aarakocra, windriders, air clerics, raiders who live by speed. Strong among tribes of the Tablelands and the Jagged Cliffs.

**Mark:** Dust that never quite settles in the hair, a scent of ozone, voices that carry unnaturally far. Some lines have pale, wind-chapped skin even in still rooms.

**On Athas:** Storm scions feel weather as mood. They move through dust and thin air where others choke. Great powers call lightning and sandstorms — useful, and a beacon to anyone watching the sky.

**Typical powers:** Wind sense, shock, flight, sandstorm veil, Storm Avatar, Silt Lightning.

---

### Sea

Water, adaptation, and healing. Sea blood is the memory of oceans Athas no longer has. It is the rarest elemental legacy in the Tablelands and one of the most hunted.

**Origin:** Water-elemental lineage from when Athas still had seas; Last Sea, underground aquifer, and silt-coast families still carry traces.

**Who carries it:** Last Sea folk, silt sailors, water clerics, oasis keepers, a few deep-cave clans. Almost never a random Tablelands peasant.

**Mark:** Cool skin in the heat, salt-white at the temples, a tendency to find water others miss. Some weep when they first see a true lake.

**On Athas:** A Sea scion who can produce water is wealth, hostage, or saint. Merchant houses and city-states will buy, bind, or kill for a known Sea line. Great powers can fake an oasis or crash a wave of force across dry ground — and every templar in earshot will hear of it.

**Typical powers:** Water sense, salt tolerance, healing spring, mist form, Rain Caller, Mirage Oasis.

---

### Stone

Earth, strength, and resilience. Stone blood is bone like bedrock, a body that does not yield, and a mind that thinks in years rather than days.

**Origin:** Earth-elemental champions or primordial giant blood that survived the Shattering in the mountains and under them.

**Who carries it:** Dwarves, muls, half-giants, miners, fortress templars, clans of the Ringing Mountains and White Mountains.

**Mark:** Stone-gray nails or knuckles, unusual weight, a stance that is hard to shift. Some lines have flecks of crystal under the skin.

**On Athas:** Stone scions endure heat, poison, and siege. They are prized as bodyguards and feared as unstoppable laborers. Great powers grow the body toward giant size or shake the ground — useful in mines, disastrous in a packed bazaar.

**Typical powers:** Stonecunning, dense bones, skin of stone, avalanche strike, Colossus Form, Earthquake Stomp.

---

### Mind

Psionics, perception, and domination. Mind blood is the Way inherited rather than trained: a lineage that thinks louder than other people.

**Origin:** Ancient psionic dynasties of the Green Age; the most common scion legacy still walking Athas. Nobles, academies, and city-state courts all claim Mind lines.

**Who carries it:** Psions, wilders, nobles, Veiled Alliance contacts, templars of thought (Nibenay's Temple of Thought, Nexus hopefuls). Any race.

**Mark:** Unblinking stare, a hush when they enter a room, headaches in the untrained nearby. Some lines have silver-gray irises.

**On Athas:** Mind is the "ordinary" Athasian bloodline — if anything about scions is ordinary. It overlaps [wild talents](psionics/README.md) without replacing them: a Mind scion may also roll a wild talent. Great powers read and break minds at a scale that makes sorcerer-kings interested in recruitment or elimination.

**Typical powers:** Empathic sense, Psionic Spark, mind reading, psychic assault, Master of the Mind, Psychic Storm.

---

### Defiler

Life-draining magic and corruption. Defiler blood is the Hemorrhage given a human shape: power stolen from living Athas and spent as spell and hunger.

**Origin:** Sorcerer-king magical experiments, catastrophic defilement at a source wound, or ancestors who stood in the ash when a city died.

**Who carries it:** Defilers, SK blood-experiments, ruined noble houses, a few who never asked for it. The DM may restrict this legacy.

**Mark:** Ash-gray fingernails, plants wilting at a touch when angry, a smell of burnt soil. Some lines cannot hide the mark when they cast.

**On Athas:** Using Defiler powers **always** advances local terrain decay unless covenant is spent on preservation. Druids, preservers, and elemental cults treat a known Defiler scion as enemy. Templars may claim them as tools. Great powers maximize spells and create zones of devastation — the same well the sorcerer-kings drink from.

**Typical powers:** Life siphon, ashen spell, life-drain aura, Devastating Defilement, Black Sun.

---

### Dragon

Dragonhood and transformation. Dragon blood is not an element. It is the path the sorcerer-kings walk: flesh rewriting itself toward the thing that ate the world.

**Origin:** Exposure to a Dragon's power, a failed or unfinished metamorphosis in an ancestor, or blood taken from a sorcerer-king's transformation. **Extremely rare.** The DM should approve this legacy.

**Who carries it:** SK heirs and experiments, survivors of a Dragon's passing, a handful of cursed bloodlines. Never common, never unnoticed for long.

**Mark:** Scale patches, slit pupils, heat in the breath, dreams of wings. Marks worsen as Great powers are used.

**On Athas:** A visible Dragon scion is a political event. Sorcerer-kings may see kin, rival, or raw material. Path to Dragonhood is a slow, permanent change toward SK-style metamorphosis — not a free epic template. Great powers include breath, fear, and partial dragon form.

**Typical powers:** Scaled skin, breath weapon, draconic strength, Partial Dragon Form, Path to Dragonhood.

## Legacy types (ranks)

**Rank** is how hard the blood hits. It is set by [Legacy Score](godshards.md), not by which of the seven legacies you have. A Weak Sun scion and a Weak Defiler scion are both Weak; their *kind* of power differs.

| Rank | LS | What it means |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Weak** | 1–10 | The blood is there and mostly asleep. No legacy powers unless the DM awakens one through trauma, rite, or Godshold. Neighbors may never know. |
| **Minor** | 11–20 | The blood works. One or two small gifts (thirst halved, a spark of fire, a whisper of thoughts). Tribe champions, minor nobles, "lucky" gladiators. |
| **Strong** | 21–30 | The blood is a reputation. Major powers (solar weapon, flight, mind reading). Merchant-house heirs, templar agents, people factions try to marry or kidnap. |
| **Great** | 31–40 | The blood is a political fact. Great powers (avatars, storms, defiling zones). City-state players; sorcerer-king attention is likely. |
| **Legendary** | 41+ | The blood is history walking. Sorcerer-king tier. Not a starting PC rank without an explicit table ruling. |

Random rank (optional): d100 — 01–50 Minor (LS 12–19), 51–85 Strong (LS 20–30), 86–98 Great (LS 31–40), 99–00 Legendary (LS 41+).

### Power tiers vs ranks

Do not confuse **rank** (how much blood you have) with **power tier** (how large each gift is):

| Power tier | What it feels like |
| --- | --- |
| **Minor power** | Always-on knack or small daily trick (resistance, sense, spark) |
| **Major power** | Combat or exploration gift used in encounters or a few times per day |
| **Great power** | Scene-changing transformation, storm, or devastation; world cost may apply |

A Strong scion (rank) might have two Minor powers and one Major power. A Great scion (rank) has Great *powers* as well. You may always take a lower-tier power than you are entitled to.

## Choosing a legacy

At character creation (or when the legacy awakens):

1. Pick one **legacy** that fits ancestry, region, or story
2. Record [Legacy Score](godshards.md) — this sets **rank**
3. Select or roll [legacy powers](blood-abilities/README.md) from that legacy's table only

**Defiler** and **Dragon** carry social stigma or SK attention. The DM may restrict them.

## Legacy mark

Many scions bear a visible **legacy mark** (sun-gold eyes, dust in the hair, crystalline knuckles, scale patches). +1 circumstance bonus on Charisma-based skills when the mark is recognized; NPCs treat the scion with respect or fear.

## Interactions with Athas systems

| System | Interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Psionics | Mind legacy overlaps; other legacies stay separate from [wild talents](psionics/README.md) unless the table merges them |
| Elemental clerics | Sun/Storm/Sea/Stone scions often serve matching element cults |
| Defiling / preserving | Defiler powers **always** carry terrain cost; other legacies may cost covenant when used for extraction |
| Dragon transformation | Dragon Great powers point toward SK-style metamorphosis (DM) |
