# Scions and Legacy Score

A **scion** carries a [primordial legacy](legacies.md) — ancient power soaked into the bloodline when the gods died. Legacy powers use Birthright-style tiers (minor / major / great) without full domain play.

## Key ability

**Charisma** sets legacy power save DCs unless a specific power says otherwise.

Base DC: 10 + ½ character level + ability modifier (Con for Sun/Stone/Defiler powers where noted; Wis for Sea; Int for Mind).

## Legacy Score ranks

**Rank** (also called **legacy type**) is how strongly the blood manifests. Flavor for each rank lives on [Primordial legacies](legacies.md#legacy-types-ranks).

| Legacy Score (LS) | Rank | Typical bearer |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1–10 | Weak | Latent blood; no legacy powers yet (or DM-granted one minor) |
| 11–20 | Minor | Minor noble, tribe champion |
| 21–30 | Strong | Merchant house heir, templar agent |
| 31–40 | Great | City-state power; sorcerer-king tier |
| 41+ | Legendary | Table-defining figure; extremely rare for PCs |

Random rank (optional): d100 — 01–50 Minor (LS 12–19), 51–85 Strong (LS 20–30), 86–98 Great (LS 31–40), 99–00 Legendary (LS 41+).

## Legacy Score and powers

LS determines how many **legacy powers** you possess (Birthright Table 2-3 structure):

| LS | Mod | Minor | Major | Great |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 12–13 | +1 | 1 | — | — |
| 14–17 | +2 to +3 | 1 | 1 | — |
| 18–19 | +4 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 20–25 | +5 to +7 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 26–29 | +8 to +9 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| 30–35 | +10 to +12 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| 36–40 | +13 to +15 | 3 | 3 | 2 |
| 41+ | +16+ | 4 | 3 | 3 |

Generate LS at creation (4d6 drop lowest, or campaign method). Weak scions (LS ≤10) have no legacy powers unless the DM grants awakening through play.

Choose powers from your [legacy table](blood-abilities/legacy-tables.md). You may take a **lower** tier than entitled (e.g. three minors instead of one major).

## Using legacy powers

Unless noted in the description:

- **Type:** Spell-like, supernatural, or extraordinary (per entry)
- **Caster / manifester level:** Character level
- **Components:** None; mental activation
- **SR / dispel:** Subject to spell resistance and *dispel magic* where spell-like
- **Antimagic:** Spell-like powers fail in antimagic fields; extraordinary abilities do not
- **Stacking:** Unnamed bonuses stack

**Defiler legacy** powers that mimic defiling always advance local terrain decay (see World cost).

## Covenant (regency-lite)

**Covenant** is loyalty and recognition channeled through a legacy cult, tribe, or faction.

**Sources:** holding recognized land or resource; proclamation by a faction; public deed matching your legacy.

**Spending:** realm-like effects (DM); **extraction** (personal power, terrain decays); **preservation** (slow decay, costly).

Unspent covenant may fade if the scion is forgotten between adventures.

## World cost

**Great** legacy powers, defiler abilities, and covenant spent on extraction reduce **local source** (DM tracks by region). Defiling, godshard extraction, and Defiler legacy powers draw from the same dying well described in the [premise](../campaign/premise.md).

## Sorcerer-kings

Canon sorcerer-kings are **Legendary** scions — usually Defiler, Dragon, or Sun legacy at highest LS. No new SK stats in these docs.

## Psionics

Legacy powers and [wild talents](psionics/README.md) are separate unless the table rules otherwise. Mind legacy **Psionic Spark** grants PP and a power but does not replace wild talent rolls.

## Selection vs random

- **Default:** Player chooses from legacy table meeting prerequisites
- **Optional:** Roll d100 on [legacy tables](blood-abilities/legacy-tables.md)
- **Hereditary:** Prefer family-known powers (~75% if rolling randomly)
