## Summary

Ravish is one of the five oasis villages located south of the Troll Grave Chasm, in the section of the Barrier Wastes called the Glowing Desert.  The community based in the village is a typical tribe of raiders -- all ex-slaves from Draj.  The tribe is ruled by Akive, a powerful mul with no leadership abilities beyond great strength, excellent combat prowess, and the ability to intimidate and bully her subordinates.  The village is the poorest, least organized of the Bandit States, and its raiders blunder their attacks as often as they successfully gain plunder.  The targets they strike with most frequency are the halfling villages of the White Mountain forests and Kurn's client villages.
