## Summary

Ko'teg is a satellite village of the kreen city J'eztere.  Similar to its parent city, Ko'teg is dominated by the lawful j'ez.  The black-shelled tohr-kreen use this village as a place to extend J'eztere's influence around Kano Swamp.<br><br>Like other tohr-kreen dwellings, Ko'teg was built using the amber-like secretions of z'ock'n.  Visitors will note that the structures are much more subdued than one will find in J'eztere or Thaythilor, with the intricate buildings staying much closer to the ground in a complex web of domes.
