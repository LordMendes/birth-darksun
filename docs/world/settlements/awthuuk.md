## Summary

Awthuuk is a satellite village of the kreen city L'rax.  Similar to its parent city, Awthuuk is dominated by the slightly evil j'hol.  The blood red tohr-kreen use this village as a place to extend L'rax's influence around Kano Swamp.<br><br>Like other tohr-kreen dwellings, Awthuuk was built using the amber-like secretions of z'ock'n.  Visitors will note that the structures are much more subdued than one will find in L'rax or Thaythilor, with the intricate buildings staying much closer to the ground in a complex web of domes.
