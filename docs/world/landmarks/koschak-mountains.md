## Summary

The Koschak Mountains are one of the only truly dominant ranges in the Crimson Savanna.  The river that runs through the Kano Swamps starts in the heights of these barren thrusts of rock.  The name comes from the kreen language and means "holder of knowledge in the sky."<br><br>The kreen tend to avoid these mountains as they have difficulty with the cold temperatures and heavy moisture that surround the peaks.  Other visitors are welcomed by the roar of the mighty mountain river as soon as they enter the stony peaks.  It is a very difficult climb to reach the mouth of the mountain river, and there are many rumors about what awaits at the end of such a journey.  Some say that it is not only the river that can shake the earth with its mighty roar.
