## Summary

Nexus is an academy of the Way located high within a small range of mountains northeast of Kurn.  The hidden site is hard to reach and well-protected by a group of powerful psionicists and their students.  A dwarf named Gulem the Gray runs the academy.  It is said that he constantly scans the reaches of Athas for psionicists worthy to study at Nexus, and that these hopefuls receive mental summons to come to the hidden facility.<br><br>Built within the mountains themselves, Nexus appears as a gray monolith beneath the crimson sun.  Due to safeguards put in place by psionicists who serve the academy, no one can find the actual location if they aren't permitted to.  In fact, making it up the mountain is one of the first tests used to determine the worth of a perspective student.  Everyone that enters Nexus is sworn to absolute secrecy concerning its location and true function.  While studying with Gulem and the other psionic masters, the neophytes must immerse themselves in the principles of the Will and the Way, mind, body and soul.<br><br>It is rumored that Gulem has binding ties to the mysterious Order.  How deep those ties go, if they indeed exist at all, are a known only to Gulem and the Order.
