## Summary

Kalidnay is the ruins of a city-state, felled several centuries ago by a long forgotten disaster. Ruled by the sorcerer-king Kalid-Ma, it was one of the richest cities of the Tyr Region. Today the ruins have been taken over by a variety of raiding tribes and dangerous monsters, which makes exploration of the devastated city a dangerous endeavor.
