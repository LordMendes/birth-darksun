# Item Creation Feats

### Brew Potion

Special:On Athas, potions take many different forms. The most common form is an enchanted fruit, often called a potionfruit. Other common items for enchantment include obsidian orbs, packs of herbs, and bone fetishes. The potion, regardless of material used to make it, is consumed or destroyed when used. Due to the nature of their magic, defilers cannot use organic materials, su ch as fruits, to create a potion. As a consequence, most non-defilers use organic receptacles almost exclusively, as a means of assuring the potion is not a product of defiler magic.

### Scribe Scroll

Special:On Athas, scrolls take many different forms. Common forms include paper or papyrus sheets, clay tablets, and woven cloth or knotted strings.
