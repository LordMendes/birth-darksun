# Ranger

> "What you call monsters and beasts are simply other beings trying to survive in the wastelands. Some of them are just as desperate, lost, and confused as you are."
> — Sudatu, elven scout

## Overview

The wastes of Athas are home to fierce and cunning creatures, from the bloodthirsty tembo to the malicious gaj. Because of this, Athasians have long learned how to adapt and survive even in the most inhospitable and savage environments. One of the most cunning and powerful creatures of the wastes is the ranger, a skilled hunter and stalker. A ranger knows their lands as if they were the ranger's home (as indeed they are); a ranger knows their prey in deadly detail.

## Making a Ranger

Skilled in wilderness survival, stealth, favored enemies or terrains, and eventually druid-like spells from lesser land spirits. **Races:** Halflings and half-elves most common; elves as tribal scouts; humans adaptable; muls after escape; pterrans and aarakocra protect villages. **Alignment:** Any; rarely lawful.

### Favored terrain (Ex)

When you could take a new favored enemy, you may instead take **favored terrain**: +2 Hide, Knowledge (nature), Move Silently, Spot, Survival in that terrain. Same progression as favored enemy (+2 increments at 5th, 10th, etc.).

**Terrain types:** Boulder Field, Forest, Jagged Cliffs, Mountain, Obsidian Waste, Rocky Badland, Salt Flat, Sandy Waste, Sea of Silt, Stony Barren, Swamp, Verdant Belt, Mud Flat, Ocean.

Example: 1st monstrous humanoid +2; 5th Rocky Badlands terrain +2 (or bump enemy to +4); 10th new enemy or +2 to existing.

## Playing a Ranger

The burnt landscape is a respected adversary; danger is constant but familiar.

## Religion

Elements, moons/stars (no priests), sorcerer-kings ([Gulg](../world/settlements/gulg.md) judaga nobles are rangers), spirits of the land (no spells unless druid multiclass).

## Other classes

Affinity for druids, barbarians, psions; slow to befriend talkative traders or templars.

## Combat

Use terrain; control spells over raw damage; Mobility, Nature's Child, Wastelander*.

## Advancement

Ranger or barbarian continuation; poison and magic weapons.

## Starting packages

**Package 1: The Archer** — Elf. Feats: Point Blank Shot, Track. Skills: Hide, Listen, Move Silently, Spot, Survival.

**Package 2: The Scout** — Halfling. Feats: Stealthy, Track. Skills: Hide, Knowledge (nature), Listen, Move Silently, Spot, Survival.

**Package 3: The Wastelander** — Thri-kreen. Feats: Track, Wastelander*. Skills: Hide, Knowledge (nature), Listen, Move Silently, Spot, Survival.
