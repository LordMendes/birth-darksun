# Wizard

> "So what if the land becomes barren? It's not like we're going to stick around."
> — Datuu Dawnchaser, elf defiler

## Overview

Athasian wizards draw energy from surrounding soil—**preservers** take only what they can without killing plants; **defilers** leave ash and sterile soil. Arcane magic and literacy are outlawed in the Seven Cities; spellbooks must be hidden.

Defiler and preserver are not separate classes—see Chapter 6 defiling rules (not duplicated here).

## Making a Wizard

Greatest strength is greatest liability—secrecy essential. **Races:** Elves and humans most common; half-elves conceal well; halflings/pterrans never willingly defile; half-giants rare and short-lived; dwarves skilled when they pursue magic; thri-kreen/muls almost never. **Alignment:** Tend lawful (secrecy, study); good preservers; neutral/evil often defilers.

## Class features

**Hit Die:** d4.

**Class skills (2 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** PHB wizard skills plus **Bluff, Disguise, and Literacy** as class skills.

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** As PHB (simple weapons; no armor without arcane spell failure).

**Spells:** Prepare arcane spells from spellbook (Int 10 + spell level; DC 10 + level + Int mod). Two spells per level plus bonus Int spells; daily slots per PHB wizard table (0–9 progression as SRD).

**Spellbook (Athas):** Illegal reading and magic force concealment—scrolls, tapestry fringes, bone staves, knot patterns, stone tablets all count as spellbooks. See DS3 Chapter 6 alternate spellbook rules.

**Familiar:** PHB familiars except **toad and weasel** (absent on Athas). Additional options:

**Scribe Scroll, familiar abilities:** As PHB.

## Playing a Wizard

Universally hunted—templars and mobs alike. Adventure to master magic, ancient lore, riches, or power.

## Religion

Often at odds with elemental clerics; preservers may become clerics; most do not worship sorcerer-kings (seeing them as advanced wizards).

## Other classes

Deadly enemies of templars; druids slay confirmed defilers; respect bards; defilers ally with paraelemental clerics.

## Combat

PHB tactics with **extra emphasis on secrecy**—defiling draws all attention. Bluff to mask casting (Chapter 3).

## Advancement

High Int and Cha; Path Dexter/Sinister, Raze feats; Bluff and Disguise; study Black/Gray/Cerulean Storm energy sources.
