# Rogue

> "Marek, always helpful, said that the UnderTyr catacombs are supposed to be haunted. Think I'll go make some inquiries about where a 'heretic' like me can get some holy earth. Always go prepared ..."
> — Janos, human rogue

## Overview

Athas is a world of intrigue, manipulation, secret deals, and subtle treachery — in short, a rogue's playground. Rather than eking out their living at the borders of society, many Athasian rogues dominate the action in some of the most powerful political factions in the Seven Cities: the Noble Houses, the templars, and the Merchant Houses. Often rogues themselves, the wealthy and powerful deploy lesser rogues as pawns in their endless games of acquisition, espionage, and deceit.

Individual rogues run the gamut of Athasian society, from the street rats of the cities to the vagabonds of the outlands, to the prosperous and respectable dune traders, to the low-ranking templars that search caravans at the gates. Accomplished rogues are often sought out by the nobility as agents, and can earn both wealth and honor in such positions — or a quick death should they be caught contemplating treachery against their masters.

## Making a Rogue

Rogues excel at exploiting openings—not standing toe-to-toe with mul warriors. **Races:** Elves, half-elves, humans; halflings, dwarves, muls adapt well; thri-kreen/pterran/aarakocra poor; half-giant rogues are comic folktale figures. **Alignment:** Lawful and chaotic equally common; lawful serve nobles/templars; good work security; evil work as assassins.

## Class features

**Hit Die:** d6.

**Class skills (8 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** PHB list plus **Use Psionic Device** (class skill). **Swim** is cross-class.

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** PHB plus **bard's friend, blowgun, garrote, small macahuitl, tonfa, widow's knife, wrist razor**. Light armor.

### Special abilities

At 5th and every three levels, choose PHB rogue special ability **or** Athasian option:

| Ability | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Dune Trader** | +4 competence Diplomacy when buying/selling; Speak Language becomes class skill |
| **False Vulnerability (Ex)** | No +4 hit when prone; kip up from prone as free action (no AoO); +4 Bluff when feinting from prone |
| **Looter's Luck (Ex)** | Move action Appraise DC 10 + items to pick most valuable in pile (full round DC 15 + items if unseen) |
| **Notoriety** | +4 Intimidate and Bluff; +4 Leadership score if you have Leadership |
| **Silver Tongue (Ex)** | Retry Bluff once at −5; +2 Disguise |

PHB options still available: crippling strike, defensive roll, improved evasion, opportunist, skill mastery, slippery mind, feat.

## Playing a Rogue

Patrons (nobles or senior templars) sponsor rogues for theft, spying, assassination—no thieves' guilds, but patronage is common.

## Religion

Superstitious but not devout; chaotic rogues favor Air.

## Other classes

Demand respect for rogue skills—rogue is an honorable profession on Athas, unlike wizard.

## Combat

Hide for surprise; feint and flank for sneak attack.

## Advancement

Bluff, Move Silently, Combat Expertise, Improved Feint; psychic warrior dip for armor/feats; dune trader prestige.

## Starting packages
