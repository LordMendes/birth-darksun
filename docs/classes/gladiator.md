# Gladiator

> "I might be a slave, but I am famous, I dine well, and my company is that of the finest noble women. Tell me, what do you have that I do not, slave trader—except the freedom to feel miserable?"
> — Jarek, arena champion

## Overview

The arena is the battlefield of the gladiator. From hand-to-hand combat in the mud pits of small forts to the grand games of the city-states, a gladiator is a warrior who fights to the sounds of people cheering his name or cursing her presence. A master of crowd control and the art of prolonged combat, gladiators are trained to fight.

Gladiators train to best wild beasts in deadly games for the amusement of the masses. They fight for glory, wealth, prestige, and power. They fight to survive. Some are merely slaves, having to fight and perhaps hoping to win a chance to obtain freedom, while some fight willingly for the thrill of combat or the promise of riches and fame.

## Making a Gladiator

Best one-on-one fighters in the Tablelands; hand-to-hand before exotic weapons; taunt and tease for crowd-pleasing fights. **Abilities:** Str and Con vital; Int for Bluff, Intimidate, Perform, Sense Motive.

**Races:** All races in arenas; muls prized; elves valued for speed and taunts; humans most common; halflings rarely (abhor slavery). **Alignment:** Any; lawful obey rules but rarely last; chaotic common. **Starting ceramic:** 4d4 × 10 (100 Cp).

## Class features

**Hit Die:** d12.

**Class skills (4 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** Balance, Bluff, Climb, Craft, Intimidate, Jump, Perform, Profession, Sense Motive, Spot, Tumble.

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** Simple and martial weapons; light and medium armor; shields (except tower).

**Mercy (Ex):** No penalty attacking for nonlethal damage (1st).

**Exotic weapon:** Bonus Exotic Weapon Proficiency at 1st, 5th, 9th, 13th, 17th.

**Unarmed strike:** Improved Unarmed Strike at 2nd.

**Arena guile (Ex):** +½ gladiator level (round down) on Bluff and Sense Motive for melee combat (2nd).

**Improved Feint:** Bonus feat at 3rd.

**Uncanny dodge / improved uncanny dodge:** As barbarian (4th, 8th).

**Armor optimization:** At 5th, 10th, 15th, 20th choose one new benefit while wearing proficient armor: +1 AC, −1 armor check penalty, +1 max Dex, or treat armor one category lighter.

**No mercy (Ex):** Coup de grace as standard action (6th).

**Parry (Ex):** Once/round forfeit highest BAB attack to opposed attack −5 vs. incoming melee (14th). **Improved parry:** No −5 (19th).

**Superior feint (Ex):** Bluff to feint as free action once/round (15th).

## Playing a Gladiator

You fight for crowd adoration as much as victory—dramatic wins matter.

## Religion

No gladiator religion; some worship sorcerer-kings or elements.

## Other classes

Arrogance toward other warriors; distrust magic; clerics seen as healers only.

## Combat

Melee revelry; tricks and feints; Toughness and arena feats (Arena Clamor, Brutal Attack).

## Advancement

Specialties: blind fighter, jazst, montare (prestige paths in DS3 appendices).
