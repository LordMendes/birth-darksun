# Druid

> "A spirit took me in, when neither of my parents would accept me. Athas provides for those who care for it. We live in a desert simply because no one cares for the land."
> — Sutura, half-elven druid

## Overview

Athasian druids protect the dying landscape, reclaim barren lands, and guard spirits of the land—often unseen until their oasis or territory is violated.

## Making a Druid

Druids cast divine spells through a land spirit's pact. **Races:** Half-elves, pterrans (Path of the Druid), aarakocra, muls, thri-kreen, halflings (Forest Ridge). Half-giants poor fit. **Alignment:** One component must be neutral (NG, LN, NE, N, CN).

## Class features

**Hit Die:** d8.

**Class skills (4 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** Concentration, Craft, Diplomacy (cc), Handle Animal, Heal, Hide*, Knowledge (nature), Listen, Move Silently*, Profession, Ride, Spellcraft, Spot, Survival, Swim.

*Hide and Move Silently are class skills; Diplomacy is cross-class.

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** PHB proficiencies plus **alak and blowgun**. Cannot use metal armor/shields (as PHB).

## Playing a Druid

Neutral guardian of Athas's balance; defilers are abominations outside life's cycle.

## Religion

Devotion to land spirits—not a shared "druid religion." Most see spirits as Athas manifest; primitives may treat spirit as god.

## Other classes

Despise wizards; templars unwelcome; elemental clerics allies; paraelemental foes; rangers best allies (sometimes conflict over favored enemy).

## Combat

Summon and wild shape for flanking and caster harassment; guerrilla ambush with woodland stride and trackless step.

## Advancement

Stay druid for companion and wild shape. Barbarian dip useful. Grove master prestige path.

## Starting packages

**Package 1: The Beastmaster** — Half-elf. Feat: Animal Affinity. Companion: jankx. Spells: 1st—cure light wounds, speak with animals; 0—cure minor wounds ×2, *defiler scent*.

**Package 2: The Defiler Hunter** — Human. Feats: Defender of the Land*, Track. Companion: jhakar. Spells: 1st—*backlash*, longstrider; 0—cure minor wounds, *defiler scent* ×2.

**Package 3: The Warden** — Pterran. Feat: Spell Focus (conjuration). Companion: lesser boneclaw. Spells: 1st—entangle, *plant renewal*; 0—*defiler scent*, detect magic, *nurturing seeds*.
