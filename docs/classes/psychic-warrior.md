# Psychic Warrior

> "The body is not bound to the forms and function you were born with. To master the art of delivering death, you must break your given mold."
> — Tharlkar, psychic sensei

## Overview

"Psychic warrior" translates thri-kreen **chakak** ("mind warrior"). Non-kreen were long called **mercenary psionicists** in the Tablelands.

## Making a Psychic Warrior

Not a typical front-liner—powers and feats define the role, like an eldritch knight of the Way. **Races:** Thri-kreen almost inevitably take at least one level; muls, humans, elves, dwarves excel; aarakocra and pterran individuals thrive; halfling/jozhal rare. **Alignment:** Usually neutral on good/evil; must be lawful or chaotic (lawful mindguards, chaotic mercenary psionicists).

## Class features

**EPH mechanics** with Athasian skill notes below.

**Hit Die:** d8.

**Class skills (2 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** Autohypnosis*, Climb, Concentration*, Intimidate*, Jump, Psicraft*, Swim (cc).

*Intimidate is a class skill. Swim is cross-class.

## Playing a Psychic Warrior

You seek martial perfection through the nexus where body, mind, and spirit meet—not faith or glory.

## Religion

May reject religion or honor Earth/Magma (strength) or Air/Sun (wisdom).

## Other classes

Best with rogues; respect fighters/bards; envy-gladiator rivalry with wizards/psions.

## Combat

Martial strikes plus melee-oriented powers; Deep Impact, Focused Sunder, Wounding Attack feat chain.

## Advancement

Power selection paramount—mix disciplines for flexibility.

## Starting packages

**Package 1: The Defender** — Mul. Feat: Combat Manifestation. Weapons: great macahuitl, javelins. Armor: scale mail.

**Package 2: The Destroyer** — Thri-kreen. Feat: Multiweapon Fighting. Weapons: gythka, chatkchas. Armor: leather.

**Package 3: The Skirmisher** — Human. Feats: Dodge, Weapon Focus (glaive). Weapons: gouge, javelins. Armor: studded leather.
