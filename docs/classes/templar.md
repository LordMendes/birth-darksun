# Templar

> "Against the law? The law is a convenience, a tool for us to use as we will, not a yoke bound around our necks. Laws are guidelines, not rules cast in iron. Stretching them is not the same as breaking them, my young apprentice. Take that to heart, for if you accuse me again, I will have your heart served cold."
> — Zelgado De'Draigee, human templar

## Overview

Templars are civil servants of the sorcerer-king's temple/bureau/order—judge, jury, executioner, tax collector, and garbage collector. Spell power is stolen from the elemental planes and granted by the monarch.

## Making a Templar

Officer casters commanding slaves, mercenaries, or undead. **Abilities:** Charisma for spells and Secular Authority; Str/Con for melee. **Races:** Humans dominate except New Giustenal; dwarves, muls, half-elves often accepted; elves less so. **Alignment:** Within one step of sorcerer-monarch (not both axes); almost never good—usually LN or LE. **Starting ceramic:** 5d4 × 10 (125 Cp).

## Class features

**Hit Die:** d8.

**Class skills (4 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** Appraise, Bluff, Concentration, Craft, Diplomacy, Forgery, Gather Information, Heal, Intimidate, Knowledge (all), Literacy, Profession, Sense Motive, Spellcraft, Spot.

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** Simple weapons; **two martial weapons**; light and medium armor; shields (except tower).

**Sigil (Sp):** Rank sigil; *arcane mark*, *purify food and drink*, *slave scent* combined 3 + Cha mod/day (not counting spell slots).

**Turn or rebuke undead (Su):** As cleric via sigil; alignment follows sorcerer-king as deity; turn as cleric **three levels lower**. 3 + Cha mod/day; +2 with 5+ Knowledge (religion).

**Ex-templars:** Displease monarch or monarch dies → lose all templar spellcasting; CR as NPC class. New patron restores abilities immediately.

## Playing a Templar

Power and influence are worthy ends; you command those drawn to your rank.

## Religion

Varies: [Hamanu](../world/settlements/urik.md) and Lalali-Puy demand worship; Nibenay and Andropinis require service only.

## Other classes

Clash with druids/elemental clerics if they call kings usurpers; insist on leading.

## Combat

Single-target and touch spells; cones need front lines; use Secular Authority out of combat.

## Advancement

Multiclass wizard or wilder (Cha); Bluff, Diplomacy, Sense Motive; Negotiator, metamagic.

## Starting packages

**Package 1: The Blaster** — Human. Feats: Combat Casting, Weapon Focus (ranged spell). Skills: Bluff, Concentration, Diplomacy, Knowledge (local), Sense Motive, Spellcraft.

**Package 2: The Controller** — Dwarf. Feat: Spell Focus (enchantment). Languages: Common, Dwarven, Elven, Saurian. Weapon: puchik.

**Package 3: The Politician** — Elf. Feat: Negotiator. Skills include Literacy. Gear: 113 Cp.
