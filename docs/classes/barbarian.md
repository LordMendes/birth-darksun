# Barbarian

> "Gith's blood! I will hunt that wizard down and skin him alive."
> — Borac, mul barbarian

## Overview

Brutality is a way of life in Athas, as much in some of the cities as in the dwindling tribes of Athas' harsh wastes. Cannibal headhunting [halflings](../races/halfling.md) (who occasionally visit [Urik](../world/settlements/urik.md) from the Forest Ridge) sometimes express shock at the savagery and bloodshed of the folk that call themselves "civilized" and live between walls of stone. They would be more horrified if they were to see the skull piles of [Draj](../world/settlements/draj.md), experience the Red Moon Hunt in [Gulg](../world/settlements/gulg.md), or watch a seemingly docile house slave in [Eldaarich](../world/settlements/eldaarich.md) rage as she finally "goes feral", taking every frustration of her short cruel life out on whoever happens to be closest at hand. Nibenese sages claim that the potential for savagery is in every sentient race, and the history of Athas seems to support their claim. Some Athasians have turned their brutality into an art of war. They are known as "brutes", "barbarians", or "feral warriors", and they wear the name with pride. Impious but superstitious, cunning and merciless, and fearless and persistent, they have carved a name for their martial tradition out of fear and blood.

## Making a Barbarian

A barbarian compensates for lack of training with powerful rage. While raging, they grow stronger and tougher; few rages per day suffice for most fights.

**Races:** Humans (wastes, escaped slaves), half-elves (abandoned to desert), muls (often "go feral" after escape), half-giants, thri-kreen and pterrans rarely (ranger/psychic warrior fit better), halflings rarely (size/strength). Elves usually from raiding tribes like the Silt Stalkers.

**Alignment:** Never lawful. City barbarians are often army rejects. Chaotic best: free and expressive at best, destructive at worst.

## Class features

**Alignment:** Any nonlawful. **Hit Die:** d12.

**Class skills (4 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** Climb, Craft, Escape Artist*, Handle Animal, Intimidate, Jump, Listen, Profession*, Ride, Survival, Swim (cc).

*Athasian additions: Escape Artist and Profession are class skills. Swim is cross-class.

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** All simple and martial weapons; light and medium armor; shields (except tower).

**Fast movement (Ex):** +10 ft. land speed in no armor, light, or medium armor (not heavy load).

**Rage (Ex):** Temporarily +4 Str, +4 Con, +2 morale on Will saves, −2 AC. Extra hp from Con lost when rage ends. Cannot use Cha-, Dex-, Int-based skills (except Balance, Escape Artist, Intimidate, Ride), Concentration, patience abilities, spellcasting, or most magic items while raging. Duration 3 + Con modifier rounds; fatigued afterward (except 17th+). Uses per day: 1 at 1st, +1 every four levels (max 6/day).

**Uncanny dodge (Ex):** Retain Dex bonus when flat-footed (2nd). **Improved uncanny dodge (Ex):** Cannot be flanked (5th).

**Wasteland Trapsense (Ex):** *Replaces PHB trap sense.* Apply trap sense bonus to Ref saves vs. natural hazards (quicksand, ant lion pits, etc.) and to AC vs. attacks by natural hazards (mekillot stampede, etc.). Bonuses +1 at 3rd and every three levels (+1/+2/+3/+4/+5/+6).

**Damage reduction (Ex):** 1/— at 7th, +1 every three levels (max 5/—).

**Greater rage (Ex):** +6 Str/Con, +3 Will (11th). **Indomitable will (Ex):** +4 Will vs. enchantments while raging (14th). **Tireless rage (Ex):** No fatigue after rage (17th). **Mighty rage (Ex):** +8 Str/Con, +4 Will (20th).

**Ex-barbarians:** Becoming lawful loses rage and further barbarian levels; retains other class features.

## Playing a Barbarian

You tap fury others fear. The wastes shaped you—family lost to tembo, village enslaved—while city-born folk know nothing of that brutality.

## Religion

Most distrust city elemental temples. Some worship Fire or Air, famous figures, or sorcerer-kings (often joining berserker units in [Urik](../world/settlements/urik.md), [Draj](../world/settlements/draj.md), or [Gulg](../world/settlements/gulg.md)).

## Other classes

Comfortable with gladiators and Air/Fire clerics. Admire bards; wary of wizards, psions, and psychic warriors (battle-loving kin).

## Combat

Choose terrain when possible; strike with power and resilience rather than fighter discipline or gladiator flair.

## Advancement

Focus on Str and speed feats: Power Attack, Whirlwind Attack, Wastelander*.
