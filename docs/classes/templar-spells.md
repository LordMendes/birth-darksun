# Templar Spells

Templars cast divine spells from this list only. When a templar gains access to a new spell level, they **automatically know every spell** of that level on this list. Casting is spontaneous (no preparation). Spells marked * are new in DS3.

See [Templar](templar.md) for casting ability (Charisma), sigil as divine focus, and domain spells from the sorcerer-monarch's assigned domains.
