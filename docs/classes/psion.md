# Psion

> "Resist all you like. I have ways of making you think."
> — Dechares, dwarven inquisitor

## Overview

The psion learns the Way—mental discipline to master the Will and manifest supernatural effects. Most cities host psionic academies where masters train students for coin or service.

## Making a Psion

Shape the Will into powers with great flexibility but a limited known-power list. **Races:** All sentient Tablelands races; sometimes non-sentient creatures. **Alignment:** Often one neutral component; any alignment possible.

## Class features

**Same as Expanded Psionics Handbook** unless noted. DS3 adds Athasian flavor only—no mechanical changes to class features.

**Hit Die:** d4. **Alignment:** Any.

**Class skills (2 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** Concentration*, Craft, Knowledge (all)*, Profession, Psicraft*; plus discipline skills (Seer: Gather Information, Listen, Spot; Shaper: Bluff, Disguise, Use Psionic Device; Kineticist: Autohypnosis, Disable Device, Intimidate; Egoist: Autohypnosis, Balance, Heal; Nomad: Climb, Jump, Ride, Survival, Swim; Telepath: Bluff, Diplomacy, Gather Information, Sense Motive).

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** Club, dagger, heavy crossbow, light crossbow, quarterstaff, shortspear; no armor or shield (armor does not interfere with manifestation).

**Power points/day, discipline, powers known, bonus feats, psicrystal:** As EPH. DC 10 + power level + Int modifier. Bonus feats at 1st, 5th, 10th, 15th, 20th (psionic, metapsionic, or psionic item creation).

## Playing a Psion

Discipline choice defines you: seer (information), shaper (constructs), kineticist (battlefield), egoist (versatility), nomad (space/time), telepath (mind control)—feared across Athas.

## Religion

Power comes from within; only animistic psions seek outside religion.

## Other classes

Drawn to fellow psions; tolerate clerics/druids; uneasy with wizards; view wilders as dangerous barbarians of the Way.

## Combat

Varies by discipline—attack, defense, and utility powers per encounter needs.

## Advancement

Choose powers carefully—fewer than a wizard's list. See [Hidden Talent](../rules/psionics/hidden-talent.md) for non-psion baseline.

## Starting packages

**Package 1: The Blaster** — Aarakocra kineticist. Feat: Overchannel. Skills: Concentration, Intimidate, Knowledge (psionics), Psicraft.

**Package 2: The Mindbender** — Human telepath. Feats: Inquisitor, Psionic Endowment. Skills: Bluff, Concentration, Gather Information, Knowledge (local), Sense Motive.

**Package 3: The Teleporter** — Elf nomad. Feat: Speed of Thought. Skills: Concentration, Jump, Psicraft, Survival.
