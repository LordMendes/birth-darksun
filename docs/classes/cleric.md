# Cleric

> "Without destruction, there is nothing to build."
> — Credo of the fire cleric

## Overview

In a world without gods, spiritualism on Athas has unlocked the secrets of the raw forces of which the very planet is comprised: Earth, Air, Fire, and Water. However, other forces exist which seek to supplant them and rise to ascendancy in their place. These forces have taken up battle against the elements of creation on the element's own ground in the form of entropic perversions of the elements themselves: Magma, Rain, Silt, and Sun.

## Making a Cleric

Clerics channel elemental energy, cast elemental spells, turn undead, and fight in armor. **Races:** All races; dwarves → Earth; elves → Water/Fire/Air; thri-kreen avoid Fire. **Alignment:** Trends only—Air/Fire often chaotic; Earth lawful; elemental clerics often good; paraelemental often evil. Exceptions abound.

## Class features

**Hit Die:** d8. **Alignment:** Any (element/paraelement does not dictate morality).

**Class skills (2 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** Concentration, Craft, Diplomacy, Heal, Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge (history), Knowledge (religion), Knowledge (the planes), Profession, Spellcraft.

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** **Light armor and all simple weapons only** (not medium/heavy—Athasian delta).

**Spells:** Prepare divine spells from cleric list (Wis 10 + spell level). DC 10 + level + Wis mod. Daily allotment per table; bonus spells for high Wis. One domain spell per level from two [Athasian domains](cleric-domains.md). One hour daily meditation/prayer to prepare.

**Domains:** Two domains from patron element's list—see [cleric-domains.md](cleric-domains.md) and Table 2-2 in README.

**Spontaneous casting:** Good clerics convert non-domain slots to cure spells; evil to inflict; neutral choose once (also sets turning).

**Turn or rebuke undead (Su):** Element worship does not change turning. Good turn/destroy; evil rebuke/command; neutral choose at creation. **All elements consider undead violations of natural order**—evil clerics may control undead but are expected to destroy them eventually. 3 + Cha mod uses/day; +2 with 5+ ranks Knowledge (religion).

**Aura (Ex):** As PHB for aligned domains/deities.

**Ex-clerics:** Gross violation costs spells and features except weapon/armor proficiencies.

## Playing a Cleric

Clerics are as varied as snowflakes—power-hungry, vengeful, preservationist, or destroyers. You expand your element's presence and oppose its foes.

## Religion

No uniform organized religion. Pacts with elemental lords or impersonal forces. Wanderers preach preservation; shrine wardens; tribal shamans; city templar-opposed preachers; noble-sponsored temple clerics. Paraelemental clerics are often feared destroyers.

## Other classes

Advisor and protector role. Distrust wizards. Elemental clerics often cooperate; opposed elements clash. Paraelemental clerics compete except Rain (preservation ally for now).

## Combat

Stay back; tactics depend on element and domains. Don't burn healing early.

## Advancement

Consider elementalist prestige class. Disciplined*, Elemental Might*, Combat Casting.

## Starting packages

**Package 1: The Defender** — Dwarf Earth cleric. Str 13, Dex 8, Con 16, Int 12, Wis 15, Cha 8. Skills: Concentration, Knowledge (religion). Feat: Disciplined*. Domains: Earthen Embrace, Mountain's Fury. Spells: 1st—*magic stone*, protection from evil, shield of faith; 0—*create element*, *detect element*, resistance.

**Package 2: The Destroyer** — Human Magma cleric. Str 14, Dex 8, Con 13, Int 10, Wis 15, Cha 12. Feat: Combat Casting, Elemental Might*. Domains: Broken Sands, Mountain's Fury. Spells: 1st—bless, divine favor, *sand pit*; 0—*create element*, resistance, virtue.

**Package 3: The Healer** — Pterran Water cleric. Wis 17. Feat: Skill Focus (Heal). Domains: Drowning Despair, Living Waters. Spells: 1st—*clear water*, protection from evil, sanctuary; 0—*create element*, detect poison, purify food and drink.
