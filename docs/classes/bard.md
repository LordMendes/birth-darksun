# Bard

> "Some people think a club can solve any problem. Unless you're a half-giant, there are more sophisticated ways of settling a disagreement."
> — Cabal, half-elven bard

## Overview

From the shadowy corners of Athas' most disreputable places hails the bard. Like their counterparts in other fantasy worlds, Athasian bards are the unquestioned masters of oral tradition and forgotten lore, but rather than sharing their lore with whoever will listen, Athasian bards guard their secrets as jealously as the sorcerer-kings harbor their water and iron. Athasian bards may sell information to the highest bidder; they peddle their services and the fruits of their knowledge, but trade secrets are what give bards an edge on the uninitiated. Bards would rather die than reveal these secrets.

Meeting a bard can be an uneasy encounter, since one never knows how the bard has chosen to devote his talents. Some bards master the art of making poisons, and survive by selling these poisons and their antidotes for those who have the coin to pay. Some bards master the art of entertainment, using their performances to amuse nobles and templars and gain wealth. Some become assassins, mixing their knowledge of poison and stealth to become hired killers.

The unique position of bards in Athasian society means they often overhear conversations between high-ranking templars or nobles, or may have treated an injured person that prefers to remain anonymous. Respectable folk despise them; the powerful fear them; but in the Athasian cities, everyone eventually comes to need their services.

## Making a Bard

Bards survive through poisons, alchemy (unique among classes), smuggling in Bard's Quarters, and performance. **Abilities:** Charisma primary; Dexterity for defense; Intelligence for skills.

**Races:** All humanoids; halfling loremasters of the Jagged Cliffs are revered, city bards are not. Elven tribes keep history-bards. Humans excel as performers or assassins. Half-elves often turn to poetry or song. Muls and half-giants make poor bards.

**Alignment:** Usually chaotic loners. Neutral bards join troupes or adventurers. Lawful bards serve templars, nobles, merchant houses. Good: entertainers and healers of poisonings. Evil: poison and alchemy dealers.

## Class features

**Hit Die:** d6. **Alignment:** Any (often chaotic).

**Class skills (6 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** Appraise, Balance, Bluff, Climb, Craft, Decipher Script, Diplomacy, Disguise, Escape Artist, Forgery, Gather Information, Heal, Hide, Intimidate, Jump, Knowledge (all), Listen, Move Silently, Perform, Profession, Ride, Search, Sense Motive, Sleight of Hand, Speak Language, Tumble, Use Magic Device, Use Psionic Device, Use Rope.

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** Simple weapons plus bard's friend, crossbow (any), garrote, greater blowgun, whip, widow's knife. Light armor only (no shields).

**Bardic music:** As PHB bardic music (countersong, fascinate, inspire courage, inspire competence, suggestion, inspire greatness, song of freedom, inspire heroics, mass suggestion)—standard action to start; Perform rank prerequisites apply.

**Poison use (Ex):** From 2nd level, never poison self when applying poison to a blade.

**Streetsmart:** +2 competence on Gather Information and Intimidate (2nd).

**Quickdraw:** Bonus feat at 3rd.

**Mental resistance (Ex):** +2 morale vs. telepathy and enchantment/charm (5th).

**Improved poison use (Ex):** Apply poison as free action, no AoO (6th).

**Quick thinking:** +2 initiative (6th); +4 (11th); +6 (16th).

**Chance:** Reroll one d20 once per day, keep second result (7th); twice per day (14th).

**Speed reactions (Ex):** When using attack/full attack in melee, subtract up to BAB from attack rolls and add same to initiative next round (9th).

**Slippery mind:** As rogue (10th). **Defensive roll:** As rogue (15th).

**Awareness (Ex):** Never flat-footed; always act in surprise round (17th).

**Mind blank (Ex):** Continuous *mind blank* effect (18th).

## Playing a Bard

You are artist and dealer—center of attention when you choose, lethal when paid. Adventure for excitement, blacklist flight, or coin.

## Religion

No central organization. Some fear elements (Air's nature). Sorcerer-king worship rare for rule-breakers.

## Other classes

Pragmatic toward all. Respect clerics/druids without awe. Fighters/gladiators/rangers as sword-arms. No wizard aversion— you sell them components.

## Combat

Skulk, strike with poison, flank with allies; bardic music supports the heavy hitters.

## Advancement

Max Bluff and Perform; invest Gather Information and Sleight of Hand. Improved Feint, Greasing the Wheels*, poisonmaster / dune trader / assassin prestige paths.
