# Fighter

> "Any wastelander can pick up a bone and call it a club, but try pitting fifty of those against one dozen trained soldiers, and maybe you'll have an even match."
> — Nikolos, human fighter

## Overview

From the small forts in sandy wastes of Athas to the guards of the merchant houses in the city-states, fighters are Athas' most common sight. Whether it is as mercenaries for the sorcerer-kings or as hired guards protecting the wealth of the nobility, fighters can be found everywhere in the Tablelands. Athas' fighters are trained both to fight in small groups and huge units. Those that have proven themselves become the commanders in the city-states' armies, leading hundreds or even thousands of men into war.

## Making a Fighter

Best fighting skills, all weapons, armor, bonus combat feats. **Races:** Humans most common; dwarves, half-giants, muls excel; thri-kreen excellent (four arms, no sleep). **Alignment:** Any.

## Class features

**Hit Die:** d10.

**Class skills (2 + Int per level, ×4 at 1st):** Climb, Craft, Handle Animal, Intimidate, Jump, Knowledge (dungeoneering), Knowledge (engineering), Knowledge (warcraft)*, Ride, Swim (cc).

*Knowledge (warcraft) is a class skill. Swim is cross-class.

**Weapon and armor proficiency:** All simple and martial weapons; all armor; all shields (including tower).

**Bonus feats:** At 1st and every even fighter level, choose a fighter bonus feat (must meet prerequisites). Common Athasian picks: Power Attack, Weapon Focus, Improved Initiative, Concentrated Fire*, Rotate Lines*, Shield Wall*, Spear Wall*, Field Officer*, Inspiring Presence*.

## Playing a Fighter

Heat makes heavy armor less desirable, but training still defines you—mercenary, guard, rebel soldier, or army commander.

## Religion

Many worship sorcerer-kings as gods or pay homage to elements before battle.

## Other classes

Respect rangers; rivalry with gladiators; welcome clerics; uneasy with wizards and templars; scorn rogues.

## Combat

Front line; maximize combat feats; archery viable with feat investment.

## Advancement

Damage feats (Power Attack, Weapon Focus) or leadership path (Field Officer*, Diplomacy cross-class).

## Starting packages

**Package 1: The Archer** — Elf. Feats: Point Blank Shot, Precise Shot. Weapons: macahuitl, dagger, longbow. Armor: chitin.

**Package 2: The Defender** — Dwarf. Feats: Disciplined, Weapon Focus (dwarven waraxe). Armor: scale mail, heavy wooden shield.

**Package 3: The Leader** — Human. Feats: Field Officer*, Inspiring Presence*, Weapon Focus (great macahuitl). Skills: Diplomacy (cc), Knowledge (warcraft), Intimidate.
