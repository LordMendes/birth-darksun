# Cleric Domains

Athasian clerics worship **elements** or **paraelements**, not gods. Domain lists below replace PHB domains. Spells marked with * are from DS3 Chapter 5.

## Table 2-2: Athasian Elements

| Element | Energy type | Domains | Typical worshipers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Air | Sonic | Desert Mirage, Furious Storm, Ill Winds, Rolling Thunder, Sky Blitz, Soaring Spirit | aarakocra, elves |
| Earth | Acid | Decaying Touch, Earthen Embrace, Forged Stone, Mountain's Fury, Ruinous Swarm | dwarves, muls, jozhals |
| Fire | Fire | Burning Eyes, Fiery Wrath, Light's Revelation, Smouldering Spirit | dwarves, ssurrans |
| Magma | Fire | Broken Sands, Dead Heat, Ill Winds, Mountain's Fury | ssurrans |
| Rain | Electricity | Cold Malice, Drowning Despair, Furious Storm, Living Waters | Drajis |
| Silt | Acid | Broken Sands, Dead Heat, Decaying Touch, Soul Slayer | silt runners, giants |
| Sun | Fire | Burning Eyes, Desert Mirage, Fiery Wrath, Light's Revelation, Sun Flare | aarakocra |
| Water | Acid | Desert Mirage, Drowning Despair, Living Waters, Sky Blitz | half-elves, lizardfolk |

Clerics choose two domains from those associated with their patron element or paraelement. See [Cleric](cleric.md).
